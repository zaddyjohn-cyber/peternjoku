Moving to Allen TX — Mortgage Guide for Relocating Buyers (2026)

RelocationBy Bond Peter NjokuJuly 25, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329), and Allen is one of the Collin County suburbs I work in most often. It's a favorite for families relocating to DFW who want top-rated schools and upscale amenities without paying Frisco or Plano prices. If you're moving to Allen, this guide covers price ranges, neighborhoods, loan programs, and how to get pre-approved before you ever set foot in Texas.

Why People Are Moving to Allen

  • Allen ISD — consistently rated among the top school districts in Texas, with strong extracurricular and academic programs
  • The Village at Allen & Watters Creek — two major upscale shopping and dining destinations within city limits
  • Allen Event Center — home to minor-league hockey and a hub for community events
  • Lower price points than Frisco — comparable schools and amenities, but median prices often $50,000–$100,000 below Frisco
  • Easy commuting — direct access to US-75 for quick trips to Plano, McKinney, and Dallas
  • No state income tax — a major draw for buyers relocating from California, Illinois, or the Northeast

Loan Programs for Allen Buyers

Conventional (Most Common in Allen)

The 2026 conforming loan limit for Collin County is $832,750. With 620+ credit and 3%–20% down, conventional is the most flexible option across Allen's price range. PMI is required with less than 20% down but cancels automatically at 80% loan-to-value. Conventional Loan details →

VA Loans — Zero Down for Veterans

Veterans with full entitlement can purchase anywhere in Allen with zero down payment and no loan limit. I'm experienced with VA loans in Collin County and can walk you through the COE process and funding fee structure. VA Loan details →

FHA Loans — Lower Credit Score Requirement

The 2026 FHA loan limit for Collin County (Allen's county) is $563,500. FHA requires just 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score, or 10% down with a 500–579 score. FHA Loan details →

Jumbo Loans — Above $832,750

For Twin Creeks Country Club or custom builds above the conforming limit, jumbo financing is available. Typically requires 720+ credit score, 10%–20% down, and reserves.

Allen Neighborhoods at a Glance

Twin Creeks

Allen's flagship golf-course community, built around Twin Creeks Country Club. Homes range from $450,000 to well over $800,000. Highly desirable for families and golf enthusiasts alike.

Watters Creek Area

Newer homes near the Watters Creek shopping and dining district. Walkable to restaurants and boutique retail, with prices typically $500,000–$700,000.

Central and South Allen

Established neighborhoods from the 1990s–2000s, offering the most affordable entry point into Allen ISD. Typically $380,000–$460,000 for 3–4 bedrooms.

Getting Pre-Approved When You're Out of State

Many Allen buyers I work with are relocating from out of state, and buying remotely is completely normal in today's market. The process:

  1. Initial call — 15–20 minutes to discuss your income, credit range, down payment, and timeline
  2. Document upload — 2 years W-2s, 30 days paystubs, 2 months bank statements, and ID via secure link
  3. Credit pull — with your authorization, I pull a tri-merge credit report and confirm your qualifying score
  4. Pre-approval letter issued — usually within 24–48 business hours, usable with any Texas Realtor
  5. Remote offers — your Realtor handles tours and offer submission, often signed entirely via DocuSign

Out-of-state income — W-2, self-employed, or remote work — is fully acceptable. Call or text me at 469-545-7180 to start.

Property Taxes in Allen

Allen (Collin County) property tax rates run approximately 2.0%–2.3% of assessed value annually. On a $470,000 home, that's roughly $9,400–$10,810/year, or about $783–$901/month added through escrow. File for the Texas Homestead Exemption at collincad.org immediately after closing.
