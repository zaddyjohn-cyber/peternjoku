First-Time Homebuyer Programs Terrell TX 2026: Down Payment Help, USDA, FHA, and Kaufman County DPA

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 20, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329) and Terrell is one of DFW's best-kept secrets for first-time buyers in 2026. Median home prices sit around $230,000–$260,000 — among the lowest in the entire metro — and much of Terrell's residential footprint is still USDA-eligible, which means qualified buyers can purchase with zero down and no monthly mortgage insurance beyond the USDA annual fee. Terrell is 30 miles east of Dallas on I-20, making it a practical commute for buyers who work in Mesquite, Garland, or anywhere along the I-20 corridor. Heartland, a growing master-planned community just west of Terrell, has added substantial new construction inventory. And Kaufman County's property tax rate of approximately 1.7% is lower than most of DFW, keeping monthly payments down. If you're a first-time buyer with a household income under $112,450 and you're priced out of Dallas County, Terrell is worth a very serious look.

What first-time homebuyer programs are available in Terrell TX in 2026?

Terrell is one of the few DFW-adjacent cities where USDA financing still applies across most of the residential area — and I always verify USDA eligibility before presenting options.

USDA Rural Development offers 100% financing — $0 down — for properties in USDA-eligible zones. Terrell's USDA household income limit for 2026 is $112,450 for a family of 1–4 (slightly different from the DPA limits) and $148,450 for families of 5–8. There's no minimum down payment, no monthly PMI (only a small annual fee of 0.35%), and a 1% upfront guarantee fee that rolls into the loan. For buyers who qualify, USDA is almost always the best loan at Terrell's price points.

TSAHC Home Sweet Texas provides a 3–5% grant (no repayment) for buyers who prefer FHA or conventional, or who are just over the USDA income limit. Income limit: $97,200 gross household (Kaufman County, 1–4 people). Pairs with FHA at Terrell's price range for very low out-of-pocket costs.

TDHCA My First Texas Home provides up to 5% deferred, forgiven at year 3. Same $97,200 Kaufman County income cap. First-time buyer or veteran required. An alternative if USDA doesn't fit your income or the specific property address.

Note: USDA eligibility is address-specific — not all Terrell properties are in USDA-eligible zones. I check your target address before you make any offer. Properties outside USDA zones can use FHA with a TSAHC grant as a strong fallback.

How much will my mortgage payment be on a home in Terrell TX?

At a $230,000 purchase price with USDA $0 down at 7.0% on a 30-year fixed (includes 1% upfront guarantee fee rolled into loan):

  • Purchase price: $230,000
  • Down payment: $0
  • USDA upfront guarantee fee (1%): $2,300 rolled into loan
  • Total loan amount: $232,300
  • Principal & interest: approximately $1,547/month
  • USDA annual fee (0.35%): approximately $68/month
  • Kaufman County property taxes (~1.7%): approximately $326/month
  • Homeowners insurance: approximately $109/month
  • Total PITI: approximately $2,050/month

With USDA, you owe only closing costs — typically $5,000–$7,500 on a $230K purchase. Ask the seller to contribute a portion and many Terrell USDA buyers close for under $1,500 out of pocket. If the property doesn't clear USDA eligibility, FHA with a TSAHC 5% grant is the next best option.

Do I qualify for down payment assistance in Terrell TX?

USDA and DPA income limits for Kaufman County in 2026:

  • USDA Rural Development: $112,450 gross household income (1–4 people) / $148,450 (5–8 people)
  • TSAHC Home Sweet Texas: $97,200 gross household income (Kaufman County, 1–4 people)
  • TDHCA My First Texas Home: $97,200 gross household income (Kaufman County, 1–4 people)

The USDA income limit is notably higher than the TSAHC/TDHCA limit — a household earning $105,000 doesn't qualify for TSAHC but may still qualify for USDA. I run both calculations on every Terrell pre-approval. A recent Terrell buyer — dispatcher, single income of $68,000, 648 FICO — purchased a $224,000 USDA-eligible home with $0 down. Closing costs were $6,200; the seller contributed $5,500 after negotiation. She closed with $700 out of pocket and a $1,980/month PITI — less than her previous rent in Mesquite.

How do I get started buying a home in Terrell?

  1. Pre-approval call (15 minutes): I pull soft credit, check income and assets, and verify whether your target address qualifies for USDA. USDA eligibility is address-specific — I check before you fall in love with a house.
  2. Formal pre-approval letter (1–2 business days): Terrell inventory is manageable but good-condition homes under $240K attract early offers. Have your letter ready before you tour.
  3. Home search (2–5 weeks): Terrell and Heartland have varied inventory from historic downtown homes to new construction. I re-underwrite quickly if you're choosing between a USDA-eligible resale and a new build.
  4. Under contract to close (35–50 days): USDA adds a USDA Conditional Commitment step that takes 1–2 weeks — budget 45–50 days from contract to close. FHA with TSAHC is faster at 35–40 days if you need to move quickly.

The most important thing I tell Terrell buyers: USDA eligibility changes as cities grow. Parts of Terrell that are USDA-eligible today may lose that status in the next map update as population grows. If you're planning to buy in Terrell with USDA, calling sooner rather than later protects your access to the program.
