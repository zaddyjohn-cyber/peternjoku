FHA Loan Arlington TX 2026 — 3.5% Down Financing in Tarrant County

FHA LoansBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 2, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329), and Arlington is one of the largest and most varied markets I work in — from established neighborhoods near UT Arlington to newer construction pushing south toward Mansfield. Between AT&T Stadium, Globe Life Field, and a genuinely broad range of price points, Arlington has room for almost every FHA buyer I meet. Here's my full 2026 breakdown for Arlington.

Why FHA Fits Arlington Buyers

Arlington home prices typically run from the low-$200,000s for established single-family homes near Lake Arlington or South Arlington up to $400,000+ in newer neighborhoods closer to Mansfield. FHA financing covers the bulk of that range comfortably. The core advantages I walk Arlington buyers through:

  • 3.5% minimum down payment — on a $290,000 Arlington home that's roughly $10,150, versus $58,000 for 20% down
  • 580+ credit score — considerably more accessible than the 620 conventional lenders typically want
  • Gift funds allowed — common for Arlington buyers getting help from family for closing costs
  • Works well with Arlington's mixed housing stock — FHA appraisal standards are manageable on both older homes and newer builds once basic repairs are addressed

FHA + Down Payment Assistance in Arlington

Arlington sits in Tarrant County, which puts buyers here in range for the Dallas/Tarrant income-limit tier — approximately $97,200 for 2026. I stack FHA with the TSAHC Home Sweet Texas grant (3–5% of the loan amount, no repayment, no first-time buyer requirement) or, for buyers closer to the Fort Worth side of the county, the Fort Worth HAP program, which offers up to $25,000 in forgivable assistance over 5 years for eligible first-time buyers. See my full down payment assistance guide for eligibility details, and my Fort Worth HAP program breakdown for the full application process.

I check TSAHC and Fort Worth HAP eligibility on every Arlington FHA pre-approval by default, since program overlap in Tarrant County is common and buyers often qualify for more than they expect.

FHA vs. Conventional in Arlington — Which Is Right?

The right choice depends on credit score and how long you plan to stay in the home. Here's the breakdown I walk Arlington clients through:

Arlington's broad range of price points means most listings I see fall comfortably within the current FHA county loan limit, which makes FHA a realistic option across the majority of the market. See my Texas FHA loan limits by county guide for the full 2026 breakdown.

Arlington Neighborhoods Where I See the Most FHA Activity

I close the most FHA loans in South Arlington and the established neighborhoods near Lake Arlington, where inventory turns over regularly and prices stay within FHA-friendly ranges. Homes closer to Mansfield and the newer subdivisions on Arlington's south side run a bit higher, so I typically run both an FHA and a conventional scenario for buyers looking in that direction to make sure they're seeing the full picture.
