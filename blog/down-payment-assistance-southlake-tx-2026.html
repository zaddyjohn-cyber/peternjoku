Down Payment Assistance in Southlake, TX (2026 Guide)

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 13, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, a licensed Mortgage Loan Officer (NMLS #2670329), and Southlake is the one DFW city where I have to give buyers a straight answer before I give them a grant program: down payment assistance works here, but only within conforming loan limits. That still covers a meaningful slice of what sells in Southlake — condos, townhomes, and homes on the more accessible end of the market. Call or text me at 469-545-7180 and I'll tell you upfront whether your target property qualifies.

Where DPA Realistically Fits in Southlake

TSAHC Home Sweet Texas and TDHCA My First Texas Home both cap the underlying loan at conforming loan limits, which cover the majority of home purchases nationwide but leave out Southlake's larger luxury estates. If you're financing above that limit, you'd be looking at a jumbo loan instead, which doesn't pair with these grant programs. For buyers targeting Southlake's more accessible inventory — townhomes, smaller lots, or properties near the edges of town — DPA is very much in play.

Southlake's County Split and Income Limits

Southlake straddles Tarrant and Denton counties, which matters because the 2026 income limit differs by side. On the Tarrant County side, the TSAHC and TDHCA limit is approximately $97,200 for a one-to-two person household. On the Denton County side, it rises to about $119,700 — the same higher tier as Collin and Rockwall counties. I confirm which county your specific address falls in before we run numbers, since it changes what you can qualify for.

A Realistic Southlake Scenario

On a $650,000 Southlake purchase that stays within conforming loan limits, a 5% Home Sweet Texas grant is $32,500 — enough to fully cover a conventional 5% down payment with money left over for closing costs. For buyers targeting Southlake's higher-end inventory above conforming limits, I shift the conversation to jumbo financing strategy instead, since a grant program simply won't apply there. Either way, I run the numbers on your specific target price before you write an offer, so there are no surprises at underwriting.

Does down payment assistance work in Southlake TX?

It can, but with a caveat — TSAHC Home Sweet Texas and TDHCA My First Texas Home both cap eligibility at conforming loan limits, so DPA applies best to Southlake buyers targeting condos, townhomes, or homes near the lower end of the local price range rather than the town's larger luxury estates. What if my Southlake home exceeds conforming loan limits?

If your loan amount exceeds the conforming loan limit, TSAHC and TDHCA grants aren't available on that transaction, and you'd typically be looking at a jumbo loan with a larger conventional down payment instead. Do I need to be a first-time buyer for Southlake down payment assistance?

Not for every program. TSAHC Home Sweet Texas has no first-time buyer requirement, so a Southlake move-up buyer within conforming limits can still qualify.
