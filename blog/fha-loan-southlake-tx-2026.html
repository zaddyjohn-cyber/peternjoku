FHA Loan Southlake TX 2026 — Where 3.5% Down Financing Fits in an Upscale Market

FHA LoansBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 5, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329), and I get asked about FHA financing in Southlake more than you'd expect for one of the DFW metro's most upscale addresses. Southlake's reputation is single-family homes on large lots with top-tier schools, and most of that inventory sits well above what FHA covers. But FHA still has a real role here for the right buyer and the right property, and I walk every Southlake client through exactly where it fits.

Where FHA Actually Fits in Southlake

Southlake's single-family home prices typically start in the $700,000s and climb from there, which puts most of the town's core inventory out of FHA's reach on its own. FHA financing works best in Southlake for:

  • Townhomes and smaller condos — a limited but real slice of Southlake's inventory near Southlake Town Square falls within FHA loan limits
  • Buyers bringing a larger down payment — a bigger down payment brings the loan amount under the FHA cap even on a higher-priced home
  • Buyers with strong income but limited savings — 3.5% down still beats a 20% conventional down payment even on a smaller Southlake purchase
  • Gift funds allowed — a parent or family member can cover the entire 3.5% down payment

FHA + Down Payment Assistance in Southlake

Southlake spans Tarrant and Denton counties, and TSAHC and TDHCA down payment assistance programs cap household income at roughly $97,200 in Tarrant County and $119,700 in Denton County for 2026. Given Southlake's price points, many households here exceed those income caps even while the home itself might qualify for FHA financing.
