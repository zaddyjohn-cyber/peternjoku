Down Payment Assistance in Mansfield, TX (2026 Guide)

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 13, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, a licensed Mortgage Loan Officer (NMLS #2670329), and Mansfield is one of the more approachable Tarrant County markets I work in for first-time buyers who want new construction without the sticker shock of Southlake or Colleyville. A TSAHC or TDHCA grant can be the piece that turns a Mansfield pre-approval into a closing. Call or text me at 469-545-7180 and I'll walk through your options.

Why Mansfield Buyers Should Look at DPA

Mansfield sits between Arlington and Fort Worth, and its mix of established neighborhoods and newer subdivisions keeps home prices more moderate than many surrounding Tarrant County cities. That makes it one of the friendlier places for a first-time buyer to stretch a grant: a 3–5% down payment grant covers a larger share of the total cash needed to close here than it would in a pricier neighboring suburb.

Tarrant County's Income Limits

Mansfield sits mostly in Tarrant County, where the 2026 TSAHC and TDHCA income limit is approximately $97,200 for a one-to-two person household — the same tier as Dallas County. I check this against your actual household income early, since it's one of the first things that determines whether a grant or a deferred loan makes more sense for your file.

A Realistic Mansfield Scenario

Mansfield's median home price typically runs $340,000–$400,000. On a $365,000 purchase, a 5% Home Sweet Texas grant is about $18,250 — more than covering FHA's 3.5% minimum down of roughly $12,775, with money left over for closing costs or a rate buydown. I run the math on both FHA and conventional structures so you can see which one nets more cash in your pocket at closing.

Timing a Mansfield Purchase Around Grant Availability

TSAHC and TDHCA both fund their programs from bond allocations that can run low late in the year, so I recommend Mansfield buyers get pre-approved and reserve their grant funding as soon as they're seriously shopping rather than waiting until an offer is accepted. I track availability on both programs and tell you upfront if either one is running tight so we can pivot to the other without losing momentum on a Mansfield listing you like.
