Moving to Prosper, Texas — Mortgage Checklist for 2026

Relocation GuideBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 14, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329), and I work with a steady stream of families relocating to Prosper from Dallas, Plano, and out of state. Prosper's median home price runs $450,000–$600,000 in 2026, Prosper ISD consistently ranks among the top districts in North Texas, and the Collin County income limit for down payment assistance sits at approximately $119,700. Here's the full checklist I walk relocating buyers through — neighborhoods, commute, HOA costs, and the exact documents you'll need for your mortgage.

What are the top neighborhoods to consider in Prosper?

NeighborhoodPrice RangeCommute (Legacy/Plano)Est. HOA

Windsong Ranch and Star Trail carry resort-style amenities — pools, trails, and sometimes an on-site elementary school — which is reflected in higher HOA dues. If amenities aren't a priority, older sections of Prosper offer a lower entry price and lighter HOA obligation.

What is the commute like from Prosper to Dallas job centers?

Most of my relocating clients are commuting to the Dallas North Tollway corridor or the Legacy Business District in Plano, which typically runs 25–40 minutes from Prosper depending on the exact neighborhood and time of day. A commute into downtown Dallas runs closer to 45–60 minutes. If your commute matters more than square footage, I steer clients toward the Prosper neighborhoods closest to the Dallas North Tollway rather than those further west.

How do HOA costs affect what I can borrow?

HOA dues count against your debt-to-income ratio the same way a car payment or credit card minimum does. A $900/year HOA fee ($75/month) reduces your qualifying loan amount by a small but real margin — often enough to matter if you're already stretching toward the top of your budget. I always ask for the specific HOA amount before finalizing a pre-approval number, rather than using a generic estimate, since Prosper's HOA fees vary meaningfully by community.

What mortgage documents do I need before moving to Prosper?

  • Two months of recent pay stubs (or year-to-date profit and loss statement if self-employed)
  • Two years of W-2s, or two years of full tax returns if self-employed or commission-based
  • Two months of bank statements for every account you'll use toward down payment or closing costs
  • Valid government-issued photo ID
  • Current lease agreement or mortgage statement, if relocating from another home you're selling or renting
  • Gift letter and donor bank statement, if any portion of your down payment is a gift

Getting these documents ready before you start touring Prosper homes shortens your pre-approval-to-offer timeline by days, which matters in a competitive market where sellers often favor the most prepared buyer.

Should I sell my current home before buying in Prosper?

This depends on whether your Prosper down payment is coming from your current home's equity. If you have enough saved separately to cover your down payment, buying first can reduce stress and give you more negotiating room. If your down payment depends on proceeds from your current sale, we may need to structure a contingent offer or a bridge strategy — I map this out with every relocating client before they start house hunting.

A recent Prosper relocation

Last month I worked with a family relocating from out of state — dual income around $145,000, strong credit in the 740s, targeting Windsong Ranch specifically for the on-site elementary school. Because they were selling their prior home simultaneously, we coordinated a rate lock and closing timeline that matched their moving truck's arrival within a two-day window, so they went from empty apartment to fully moved into their new Prosper home without a gap in housing.
