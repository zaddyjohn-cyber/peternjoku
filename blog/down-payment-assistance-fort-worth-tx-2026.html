Down Payment Assistance in Fort Worth, TX (2026 Guide)

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuJuly 28, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, a licensed Mortgage Loan Officer (NMLS #2670329), and while my office is in Garland, I close loans across Tarrant County regularly — and Fort Worth has one of the best city-run down payment programs in all of DFW. If you're renting in Fort Worth and assume you need tens of thousands of dollars saved before you can buy, I want to walk you through what's actually available in 2026, because the Fort Worth Homebuyer Assistance Program alone can change the entire math.

Fort Worth HAP: The City's Own $25,000 Program

Fort Worth runs its own Homebuyer Assistance Program (HAP), offering up to $25,000 in down payment and closing cost help for income-eligible first-time buyers purchasing within city limits. The assistance is structured as a forgivable loan over five years — stay in the home as your primary residence for the full term and you owe nothing back. This is one of the more generous city-level programs in DFW, and it's specific to Fort Worth, meaning buyers in neighboring Tarrant County cities don't have access to this exact program.

Income Limits and Qualifying Factors

Tarrant County's 2026 income limit for these programs is approximately $97,200 for a one-to-two person household, with higher limits for larger families. Credit-wise, I generally need a 620 minimum FICO score to pair Fort Worth HAP or a state grant with FHA financing, though exceptions exist for strong files.

What This Looks Like on a Real Fort Worth Purchase

On a $275,000 Fort Worth home, the full $25,000 HAP allocation covers well beyond the FHA minimum down payment of roughly $9,625, leaving the remainder available for closing costs, prepaid items, or even a temporary interest rate buydown. I map this out for every Fort Worth client before we start touring homes so there are no surprises when we get to the closing table.
