Why FHA Fits Lewisville's Market

Lewisville sits mostly in Denton County with a slice in Dallas County, and offers a wider price range than neighboring Flower Mound — from established neighborhoods near Old Town to newer construction closer to Highland Village. That range makes FHA's 580 minimum credit score and 3.5% down payment a natural fit for buyers who've been renting nearby and want to stay in the area without needing conventional-level credit or a large down payment.

FHA Loan Limits for Lewisville, TX in 2026

Lewisville uses the standard Dallas-Fort Worth metro FHA loan limit for 2026, which applies regardless of whether your specific property sits in the Denton County or Dallas County portion of the city. This limit covers the majority of homes on the market in Lewisville, including most properties near Lake Lewisville and the Vista Ridge area.

Stacking Down Payment Assistance With FHA in Lewisville

FHA's low 3.5% down requirement pairs cleanly with down payment assistance programs like TSAHC's Home Sweet Texas grant or TDHCA's My First Texas Home. I structure this combination often for Lewisville buyers, and in many cases the grant covers the entire down payment, leaving only closing costs to plan for.

What Lewisville Buyers Should Know About FHA Appraisals

FHA loans require an FHA-approved appraisal that checks for both value and basic safety standards. Given Lewisville's mix of older established neighborhoods and newer builds, I make sure every client understands what the appraiser is looking for before the inspection, so nothing derails your closing timeline.
