VA Loan Richardson TX 2026: $0 Down for Veterans & Active Duty

VA LoansBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 3, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, and Richardson is a market I get a lot of VA loan questions about, largely because it sits right next to the telecom corridor and draws veterans working in tech, defense contracting, and at nearby UT Dallas. Home prices here run a bit higher than Garland or Mesquite, and I regularly hear from veterans who assume that means a bigger down payment. It doesn't. Here's what I explain to every Richardson veteran who calls or texts me at 469-545-7180.

Why VA Loans Make Sense in a Pricier Market Like Richardson

VA loans are backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs and open to eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and certain surviving spouses. The core benefit is $0 down — no percentage requirement at all for borrowers with full entitlement. There's also no monthly PMI or MIP charge, which matters even more in Richardson than in lower-priced markets: on a $420,000 home, skipping mortgage insurance can save $150-$250 a month compared to FHA or a conventional loan with less than 20% down.

Because VA loans have no federally set maximum loan amount for borrowers with full entitlement, Richardson's higher price point isn't the obstacle veterans often assume it is. As long as your income supports the payment, the financing works the same as it would in a lower-cost city.

Entitlement, Funding Fee, and What They Mean for You

Most first-time VA borrowers have full entitlement, which means there's no cap on the $0-down loan amount as long as your income and debt-to-income ratio support it. If you've used a VA loan before, I pull your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) to check exactly how much entitlement remains, since that affects whether a subsequent purchase in Richardson requires any down payment at all. Veterans with a VA disability rating also get a full waiver on the funding fee, which on a $420,000 loan is a savings of roughly $9,000 upfront.

What to Expect During the VA Loan Process

  • COE and pre-approval: I pull your Certificate of Eligibility, verify income, and issue a pre-approval letter — typically within 24 hours.
  • House hunting: Richardson agents and sellers are familiar with VA financing, so your offer is treated the same as a conventional buyer's.
  • VA appraisal: The VA requires its own appraisal confirming the home meets Minimum Property Requirements. Richardson's housing stock, a mix of older ranch homes and newer builds near CityLine, generally passes without issue.
  • Closing: Most VA files I close in Richardson take 30-35 days from contract to keys.

VA vs. Conventional for Richardson Veterans

In a market where home prices run higher, the no-PMI advantage of a VA loan matters even more. Conventional loans with less than 20% down can carry $150+ monthly in mortgage insurance on a Richardson-priced home, on top of requiring a down payment VA doesn't. Unless entitlement issues or funding fee costs change the math for your specific situation, VA financing is almost always the stronger option for eligible buyers here. I run the full comparison before recommending either path.
