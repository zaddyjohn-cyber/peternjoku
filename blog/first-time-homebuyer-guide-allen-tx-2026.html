First-Time Homebuyer Programs Allen TX 2026: Down Payment Grants and FHA Options for Collin County Buyers

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 15, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329) and I help first-time buyers close on homes in Allen every month. Allen ISD is A-rated by the Texas Education Agency, Watters Creek is a thriving walkable district, and Allen sits squarely in Collin County — which gives buyers access to some of the best first-time homebuyer programs in Texas. Median home prices run $380,000–$470,000, comfortably below the 2026 FHA loan limit of $563,500, and the Collin County DPA income limit of $119,700 keeps most two-income Allen households eligible for grant assistance. Here's how to use those programs effectively.

What first-time homebuyer programs are available in Allen TX?

Allen buyers have access to two major state DPA programs I routinely stack together:

TSAHC Home Sweet Texas — a grant of 3–5% of the loan amount that never has to be repaid. No second lien, no clawback if you sell. On a $390,000 Allen purchase with FHA (3.5% down = $13,650), a 5% TSAHC grant of $19,500 covers the down payment in full with money toward closing. Requires a 620 FICO and income at or below $119,700 for a Collin County household of 1–4 people. No first-time buyer requirement.

TDHCA My First Texas Home — up to 5% deferred, forgiven at year 3 if you stay in the home. Same $119,700 income cap and 620 credit floor. Requires first-time buyer status (or veteran with no ownership in 3 years). Works with FHA, conventional, and VA loans.

Allen's prices make these programs exceptionally effective — because the median is lower than Frisco or Plano, the same income and credit qualifications go further here, and cash-to-close is genuinely manageable for dual-income households in the $80,000–$115,000 range.

FHA is the standard path for Allen first-time buyers at 580–679 FICO, especially with TSAHC layered on top. Buyers at 680+ who expect to build equity quickly often choose conventional — the PMI will cancel once you reach 80% LTV, and the total cost over 7–10 years is often lower. Veterans should always look at VA first: zero down, no monthly MIP, and no DPA income cap.

What will my mortgage payment be on an Allen TX home?

At Allen's median of roughly $390,000, here's a 5% down FHA purchase at 7.0% on a 30-year fixed:

  • Purchase price: $390,000
  • Down payment (5%): $19,500
  • Loan amount: $370,500
  • Principal & interest: approximately $2,465/month
  • FHA annual MIP (0.55%): approximately $170/month
  • Collin County property taxes (~1.5%): approximately $488/month
  • Homeowners insurance: approximately $145/month
  • Total PITI: approximately $3,268/month

With a TSAHC 5% grant ($19,500), the down payment is fully covered. Closing costs on a $390,000 purchase typically run $6,500–$8,500. FHA allows sellers to pay up to 6% toward closing — that's $23,400 — so there's plenty of room to negotiate seller credits that bring your cash-to-close down to $3,000–$5,000.

Do I qualify for DPA in Allen TX in 2026?

For Collin County DPA programs in 2026, the income limit is $119,700 gross household income (1–4 people). Most dual-income Allen households I speak with are comfortably within this range. Even households earning up to $119,000 qualify — which covers many teachers, nurses, first responders, and tech professionals early in their careers.

I recently helped an Allen couple — one marketing manager and one dental hygienist — earning a combined $103,000. Their 651 FICO put them squarely in the FHA lane, and TSAHC's 5% grant ($18,750 on their $375,000 purchase) zero'd out the down payment. The seller covered $7,000 in closing costs, and they moved in having spent $4,400 total out of pocket. Their PITI of $3,150/month was $200/month less than the rent on the apartment they'd been in for two years.

What's the step-by-step process for buying a first home in Allen?

  1. Pre-approval (same day): I review income, credit, and assets in a 15-minute call and tell you immediately which programs you qualify for — including TSAHC and TDHCA eligibility.
  2. Pre-approval letter (1–2 business days): Allen's market moves fast. You need a letter before making offers — and DPA pre-approval specifically so your realtor can present it with the offer.
  3. Home search (2–8 weeks): Allen has better inventory than Frisco or Plano at similar price points. Most buyers find the right home within 30–60 days.
  4. Under contract to close (30–45 days): FHA with DPA closes in 35–45 days. I keep the process on track and communicate directly with your realtor throughout.
