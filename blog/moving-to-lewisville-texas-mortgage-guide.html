Moving to Lewisville TX — Mortgage Guide for Relocating Buyers (2026)

RelocationBy Bond Peter NjokuJuly 26, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329), and Lewisville is one of the Denton County suburbs I work in regularly. It draws relocating buyers who want lake living, an affordable price point, and a location smack in the middle of the Metroplex — close to Dallas, Fort Worth, and DFW Airport all at once. If you're moving to Lewisville, this guide covers price ranges, neighborhoods, loan programs, and how to get pre-approved before you ever set foot in Texas.

Why People Are Moving to Lewisville

  • Lake Lewisville — 29,000-acre reservoir with marinas, parks, and waterfront living inside city limits
  • Central location — quick access to I-35E, I-35W, and the Sam Rayburn Tollway, with Dallas, Fort Worth, and DFW Airport all within 30 minutes
  • Lewisville ISD — a large, well-regarded school district serving most of the city
  • Affordability — noticeably lower price points than neighboring Flower Mound, Southlake, or Highland Village
  • Retail and dining — Music City Mall, Vista Ridge Mall corridor, and MedCity health campus
  • No state income tax — a major draw for buyers relocating from California, Illinois, or the Northeast

Loan Programs for Lewisville Buyers

Conventional (Most Common in Lewisville)

The 2026 conforming loan limit for Denton County is $832,750. With 620+ credit and 3%–20% down, conventional is the most flexible option across Lewisville's price range. PMI is required with less than 20% down but cancels automatically at 80% loan-to-value. Conventional Loan Lewisville details →

FHA Loans

The 2026 FHA loan limit for Denton County is $563,500. FHA requires just 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score, or 10% down with a 500–579 score, and covers most of Lewisville's entry and mid-range market. FHA Loan Lewisville details →

VA Loans — Zero Down for Veterans

Veterans with full entitlement can purchase anywhere in Lewisville with zero down payment and no loan limit. I'm experienced with VA loans in Denton County and can walk you through the COE process and funding fee structure. VA Loan details →

Lewisville Neighborhoods at a Glance

Castle Hills

A large master-planned community with resort-style amenities, golf, and a mix of home sizes. Popular with families, typically $400,000–$650,000.

Old Town Lewisville

Walkable historic downtown with converted lofts, shops, and restaurants nearby. Home prices range from $280,000–$420,000 depending on renovation level.

Near Lake Lewisville

Communities close to the marinas and shoreline parks, popular with buyers wanting weekend boating access without a long drive. Typically $350,000–$550,000.

Getting Pre-Approved When You're Out of State

Many Lewisville buyers I work with are relocating for jobs anywhere in the Metroplex, thanks to the city's central location, and buying remotely is completely normal in today's market. The process:

  1. Initial call — 15–20 minutes to discuss your income, credit range, down payment, and timeline
  2. Document upload — 2 years W-2s, 30 days paystubs, 2 months bank statements, and ID via secure link
  3. Credit pull — with your authorization, I pull a tri-merge credit report and confirm your qualifying score
  4. Pre-approval letter issued — usually within 24–48 business hours, usable with any Texas Realtor
  5. Remote offers — your Realtor handles tours and offer submission, often signed entirely via DocuSign

Out-of-state income — W-2, self-employed, or remote work — is fully acceptable. Call or text me at 469-545-7180 to start.

Property Taxes in Lewisville

Lewisville (Denton County) property tax rates run approximately 2.0%–2.4% of assessed value annually. On a $400,000 home, that's roughly $8,000–$9,600/year, or about $667–$800/month added through escrow. File for the Texas Homestead Exemption at dentoncad.com immediately after closing.
