Down Payment Assistance in Richardson, TX (2026 Guide)

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 9, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, a licensed Mortgage Loan Officer (NMLS #2670329), and Richardson is a market I get a lot of questions about because it straddles two very different DFW income-limit zones. The city itself sits in Dallas County, but it borders Collin County, and I regularly work with buyers who assume the higher Collin County limit applies just because Richardson feels like a Collin County suburb. Getting this right matters, because it changes whether you qualify.

Dallas County's Income Limits Apply Here

Dallas County's 2026 income limit for TSAHC and TDHCA assistance is approximately $97,200 for a one-to-two person household. Because Richardson is legally in Dallas County, this is the number I check first — not the roughly $119,700 limit used just across the border in Collin County. I've seen buyers get their hopes up assuming the higher figure applied to their Richardson address, so confirming this upfront saves a lot of confusion later in the process.

Richardson's established neighborhoods typically run $336,000–$420,000, so a 5% grant means roughly $16,800–$21,000 toward your down payment. I also check whether a specific Richardson address happens to fall inside the City of Dallas's DHAP boundary, since that program's up-to-$60,000 forgivable loan is a much bigger number than TSAHC or TDHCA when it applies. Most Richardson addresses don't qualify for DHAP, but it's always worth checking before ruling it out.

A Realistic Richardson Scenario

On a $420,000 Richardson home, a 5% Home Sweet Texas grant is $21,000. Paired with FHA's 3.5% down payment of $14,700, that grant covers your entire down payment with about $6,300 left toward closing costs. If your household income is close to the $97,200 Dallas County limit, I run the numbers early so we're not surprised mid-contract — that's a conversation I'd rather have before you're under a deadline.
