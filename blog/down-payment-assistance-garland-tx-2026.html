Garland's Position in the DFW Down Payment Landscape

Garland benefits from being in Dallas County, which means buyers here can layer statewide grant programs with, in some cases, county-level assistance depending on the specific property and lender partnerships in place at the time of your purchase. I check every layer available for a Garland address before I quote a client, because the difference between a 3% grant and a fully-stacked package can be thousands of dollars.

Income Limits and Credit Requirements

Dallas County's 2026 income limit for these programs sits around $97,200 for smaller households, with adjustments upward for larger families. On the credit side, I need a 620 minimum FICO to pair assistance with FHA, though I've closed exceptions as low as 600 with strong compensating factors like low debt or a longer job history.

Frequently Asked Questions

What down payment assistance programs work in Garland TX in 2026?

Garland buyers typically use TSAHC Home Sweet Texas (a 3-5% grant), TDHCA My First Texas Home (up to 5%, forgiven at year three), and depending on the property location within Dallas County, sometimes county-level HOME funds. I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329) and I run the eligibility check for you — call or text me at 469-545-7180.

How much down payment assistance can a Garland buyer get?

Most Garland buyers I work with qualify for 3-5% of their loan amount in grant or deferred-loan assistance, which on a typical $260,000-$300,000 Garland home is roughly $8,000-$15,000. I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329) — call or text me at 469-545-7180 and I'll calculate your exact number.

Is down payment assistance in Garland only for first-time buyers?

Not always. TSAHC Home Sweet Texas has no first-time buyer requirement, so repeat buyers in Garland can use it too. TDHCA My First Texas Home does require first-time buyer status unless you're a veteran.
