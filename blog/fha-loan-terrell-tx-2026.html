Terrell has a walkable historic downtown, some of the most affordable home prices left in the DFW metro, and a growing new-construction footprint. Because Terrell is also USDA-eligible, I always run both FHA and USDA scenarios for buyers here so you get the loan that actually costs less.

Why FHA Fits Terrell Buyers

Terrell home prices typically run from the $200,000s for established central homes up to the $400,000s in newer construction on the town's edges. FHA financing covers virtually all of that range, and it's often the difference between renting another year and closing on a house this year. The core advantages:

  • 3.5% minimum down payment — on a $280,000 Terrell home that's roughly $9,800, versus $56,000 for 20% down
  • 580+ credit score — considerably more accessible than the 620 conventional lenders typically want
  • Gift funds allowed — a parent or family member can cover the entire down payment
  • No income cap — unlike USDA, FHA has no household income limit, so higher earners still qualify

FHA vs. USDA in Terrell — Which Saves You More?

Terrell sits in Kaufman County, which is USDA-eligible, meaning income-qualified buyers can often get $0 down instead of FHA's 3.5%. But USDA isn't automatically the better deal — it comes with its own guarantee fee and household income caps. I run both scenarios for every Terrell client:

If your income is under the Kaufman County USDA limit and your credit is solid, USDA's $0 down often wins. If your income is above the limit, your credit is in the high-500s or low-600s, or the specific address doesn't map as USDA-eligible, FHA is usually the stronger path.
