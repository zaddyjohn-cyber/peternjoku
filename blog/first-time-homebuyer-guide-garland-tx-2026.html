First-Time Homebuyer Programs Garland TX 2026: Down Payment Help, FHA, and Dallas County DPA

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 18, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329) and I help first-time buyers close on homes in Garland year-round. Garland is home base for my practice — my office is at 1919 S. Shiloh Rd in the heart of the city — and I've helped buyers in South Garland, Duck Creek, and Firewheel close with DPA grants stacked on FHA to minimize out-of-pocket costs. Median home prices run roughly $250,000–$330,000, well inside the 2026 FHA loan limit of $563,500, and Garland sits in Dallas County where the income limit for down payment assistance programs is $97,200. Garland also has its own Housing Finance Corporation offering mortgage credit certificates — a tax credit layered on top of any DPA grant. If you're renting in Garland right now and want to know whether owning costs you more or less per month, call me before you assume.

What first-time homebuyer programs are available in Garland TX in 2026?

Garland first-time buyers have more stacked DPA options than most DFW cities — and I run all of them before recommending the combination that lowers your out-of-pocket costs most.

Dallas DHAP is the headline program for properties inside Dallas city limits, offering up to $60,000 forgivable over 5 years. Most Garland addresses are outside Dallas proper, so DHAP usually doesn't apply to Garland purchases — but it's worth verifying your specific address at the Dallas Office of Fair Housing. For Garland buyers, TSAHC and TDHCA are the primary levers.

TSAHC Home Sweet Texas provides a 3–5% grant — real grant money, no second lien, never repaid. On a $295,000 Garland purchase, a 5% TSAHC grant of $14,200 fully covers the $10,325 FHA down payment with $3,875 left toward closing costs. Income limit: $97,200 gross household (Dallas County, 1–4 people). Minimum credit score: 620.

TDHCA My First Texas Home offers up to 5% as a deferred second loan forgiven after 3 years. Same $97,200 income limit. Pairs with FHA, conventional, or VA. First-time buyer status required (or veteran).

Garland Housing Finance Corporation (HFC) offers a $10,000 second lien down payment program plus a Mortgage Credit Certificate — a tax credit of up to $2,000 per year, every year you're in the home. The MCC alone can save $6,000 over three years on top of whatever DPA you stack. HFC programs have their own income and purchase price limits by family size — I run the eligibility check for free.

Note: Dallas DHAP (up to $60,000 forgivable) applies to properties inside Dallas city limits — not all Garland addresses qualify. I verify eligibility before you make any offer.

Veterans should look at VA first — $0 down and no monthly MIP are the strongest combination when you qualify.

How much will my mortgage payment be on a home in Garland TX?

At Garland's current median of roughly $295,000, here's what a 3.5% down FHA purchase looks like at a 7.0% rate on a 30-year fixed:

  • Purchase price: $295,000
  • Down payment (3.5%): $10,325
  • Loan amount: $284,675
  • Principal & interest: approximately $1,895/month
  • FHA annual MIP (0.55%): approximately $130/month
  • Dallas County property taxes (~2.0%): approximately $492/month
  • Homeowners insurance: approximately $130/month
  • Total PITI: approximately $2,647/month

Stack a TSAHC 5% grant ($14,200) and your down payment is fully covered. Closing costs typically run $6,500–$9,000 on a $295,000 purchase — ask the seller to contribute up to 6% of the purchase price toward closing costs and you may close for under $1,000 out of pocket.

Do I qualify for down payment assistance in Garland TX?

The income thresholds for Dallas County DPA programs in 2026:

  • TSAHC Home Sweet Texas: $97,200 gross household income (1–4 people), 115% AMI (slightly higher for some zip codes)
  • TDHCA My First Texas Home: $97,200 gross household income (1–4 people)
  • Garland HFC: income limits vary by family size — typically $80,000–$110,000 depending on household size

Most Garland first-time buyers I work with earn $55,000–$90,000 — comfortably within the Dallas County limit. The DPA income limit is one of the easier thresholds to clear in DFW. A recent client near Firewheel — single income of $72,000, 638 FICO — qualified for FHA plus a TSAHC 5% grant. We stacked the Garland HFC MCC on top, giving her a $2,000/year tax credit. She closed at $289,000 with $2,100 out of pocket after seller-paid closing costs.

How do I get started buying a home in Garland?

  1. Pre-approval call (15 minutes): I pull soft credit, check your income and assets, and tell you the exact programs you qualify for that day — including the Garland HFC MCC if you're eligible.
  2. Formal pre-approval letter (1–2 business days): What you hand your realtor. In South Garland and Firewheel, move-in-ready homes under $280K attract multiple offers — a letter is non-negotiable.
  3. Home search (2–6 weeks): Garland has solid inventory at most times of year. I stay available to re-underwrite quickly if you find something above or below your initial price target.
  4. Under contract to close (35–45 days): FHA with DPA and HFC paperwork adds a few days versus a straight FHA close. I coordinate directly with the title company and Garland HFC when the MCC is involved.

The most common mistake I see Garland buyers make is waiting until they "feel ready" rather than getting pre-approved first. The pre-approval is what tells you what ready actually looks like — how much home you qualify for, which DPA programs you access, and exactly what your monthly payment will be.
