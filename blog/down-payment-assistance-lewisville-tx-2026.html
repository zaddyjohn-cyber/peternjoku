Down Payment Assistance in Lewisville, TX (2026 Guide)

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 9, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, a licensed Mortgage Loan Officer (NMLS #2670329), and Lewisville is a market I like recommending to buyers who want Denton County schools and a quick commute into Dallas without Flower Mound or Southlake price tags. The one thing that stops a lot of them from moving forward is the down payment — which is usually a smaller obstacle than they think once we look at Denton County's grant programs.

Denton County's Income Limits

Denton County's 2026 income limit for TSAHC and TDHCA assistance is approximately $119,700 for a one-to-two person household — the same tier as Collin and Rockwall counties, and meaningfully higher than the roughly $97,200 limit in Dallas or Tarrant County. That extra headroom helps a lot of Lewisville households qualify who might not in a Dallas County suburb at the same income.

Lewisville's home prices commonly run $312,000–$390,000, so a 5% grant means roughly $15,600–$19,500 toward your down payment and closing costs. I pair the grant with either an FHA loan for buyers still building credit, or conventional financing for buyers with a 680+ score who want to avoid FHA's mortgage insurance for the life of the loan.

A Realistic Lewisville Scenario

On a $390,000 Lewisville home, a 5% Home Sweet Texas grant is $19,500. Paired with FHA's 3.5% down of $13,650, that grant covers the full down payment with about $5,850 left toward closing costs. If you're relocating for work and haven't owned in the past three years, you likely qualify as a first-time buyer for TDHCA too, which opens up a second program to compare against TSAHC.
