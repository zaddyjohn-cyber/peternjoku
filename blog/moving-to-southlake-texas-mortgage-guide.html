Moving to Southlake TX — Mortgage Guide for Relocating Buyers (2026)

RelocationBy Bond Peter NjokuJuly 27, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329), and Southlake is one of the most requested relocation destinations I work with in DFW. It draws executives, dual-income professionals, and families who prioritize top-ranked schools and are willing to pay for it. If you're moving to Southlake, this guide covers price ranges, Carroll ISD, jumbo vs. conforming financing, and how to get pre-approved before you ever set foot in Texas.

Why People Are Moving to Southlake

  • Carroll ISD — one of the highest-ranked public school districts in Texas, a major draw for relocating families
  • Southlake Town Square — an upscale outdoor shopping, dining, and events district that functions as the city's downtown
  • Proximity to DFW Airport — a top reason for relocating executives who fly frequently
  • Master-planned neighborhoods — large lots, custom builds, and mature tree canopy throughout much of the city
  • Corporate presence nearby — close to major employers in Westlake, Grapevine, and North Fort Worth
  • No state income tax — a significant factor for buyers relocating from California, Illinois, or the Northeast

Loan Programs for Southlake Buyers

Conventional and Jumbo (Most Common in Southlake)

The 2026 conforming loan limit for Tarrant and Denton counties is $806,500. A meaningful share of Southlake purchases exceed that and require jumbo financing, which I arrange through my lending partners with competitive rates and flexible documentation for executive and self-employed income. FHA Loans

The 2026 FHA loan limit for Tarrant County is $524,225. FHA requires just 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score, and while it covers a smaller share of Southlake's inventory given the price points here, it remains an option for entry-priced resale homes. FHA Loan Southlake details →

Veterans with full entitlement can purchase in Southlake with zero down payment and no VA-imposed loan cap, though jumbo VA underwriting applies above the conforming limit. Southlake Neighborhoods at a Glance

Timarron

An established golf-course community with a mix of resale homes on large lots, popular with families wanting mature landscaping. Generally $700,000–$1,100,000.

Southlake Town Square Area

Central location within walking distance of shopping and dining, mixing townhomes with nearby single-family neighborhoods. Typically $600,000–$1,200,000.

North Southlake / Carroll ISD Core

Premium custom and semi-custom homes zoned to the most in-demand Carroll ISD elementary and middle schools. Generally $1,200,000 and up.

Getting Pre-Approved When You're Out of State

Many Southlake buyers I work with are relocating executives on a tight corporate timeline, and buying remotely is completely normal in today's market. The process:

  1. Initial call — 15–20 minutes to discuss your income, credit range, down payment, and timeline
  2. Document upload — 2 years W-2s or tax returns, 30 days paystubs, 2 months bank and asset statements, and ID via secure link
  3. Credit pull — with your authorization, I pull a tri-merge credit report and confirm your qualifying score
  4. Pre-approval letter issued — usually within 24–48 business hours, usable with any Texas Realtor
  5. Remote offers — your Realtor handles tours and offer submission, often signed entirely via DocuSign

Out-of-state income — W-2, self-employed, executive compensation, or relocation packages — is fully acceptable. Call or text me at 469-545-7180 to start.

Property Taxes in Southlake

Southlake (Tarrant and Denton counties) property tax rates run approximately 2.0%–2.4% of assessed value annually, depending on the specific school and municipal utility district. On a $1,000,000 home, that's roughly $20,000–$24,000/year, or about $1,667–$2,000/month added through escrow. File for the Texas Homestead Exemption at tad.org (Tarrant Appraisal District) immediately after closing.
