Moving to Rockwall TX — Mortgage Guide for Relocating Buyers (2026)

RelocationBy Bond Peter NjokuJuly 25, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329), and Rockwall is one of my favorite areas to work in — it's one of the fastest-growing counties in Texas, built around Lake Ray Hubbard, and it draws relocating buyers who want a lake-town feel without giving up easy access to Dallas. If you're moving to Rockwall, this guide covers price ranges, neighborhoods, loan programs, and how to get pre-approved before you ever set foot in Texas.

Why People Are Moving to Rockwall

  • Lake Ray Hubbard — Rockwall sits directly on the lake's eastern shore, with marinas, waterfront parks, and boating access uncommon in most DFW suburbs
  • The Harbor — a lakefront entertainment district with restaurants, shops, and a marina, giving Rockwall a resort-town feel
  • Rockwall ISD — a highly rated school district serving the majority of the city
  • Fast growth, still manageable — Rockwall County is among the fastest-growing in Texas, with new construction active throughout the city
  • Commute to Dallas — roughly 30 minutes to downtown Dallas via I-30
  • No state income tax — a major draw for buyers relocating from California, Illinois, or the Northeast

Loan Programs for Rockwall Buyers

Conventional (Most Common in Rockwall)

The 2026 conforming loan limit for Rockwall County is $832,750, covering most Rockwall purchases including many lake-area homes. With 620+ credit and 3%–20% down, conventional is the most flexible option here. PMI is required with less than 20% down but cancels automatically at 80% loan-to-value. Conventional Loan details →

VA Loans — Zero Down for Veterans

Veterans with full entitlement can purchase anywhere in Rockwall with zero down payment and no loan limit. I'm experienced with VA loans in Rockwall County and can walk you through the COE process and funding fee structure. VA Loan details →

FHA Loans

The 2026 FHA loan limit for Rockwall County is $563,500. FHA requires just 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score, or 10% down with a 500–579 score, and covers most non-lakefront Rockwall inventory. FHA Loan details →

Jumbo Loans — Above $832,750

For direct lakefront homes or custom estates above the conforming limit, jumbo financing is available. Typically requires 720+ credit score, 10%–20% down, and reserves.

Rockwall Neighborhoods at a Glance

The Shores / Lake Ray Hubbard Waterfront

Rockwall's premium lakefront and lake-view neighborhoods, with homes ranging from $600,000 to well over $1 million depending on water frontage.

Buffalo Creek / South Rockwall

Newer master-planned communities with resort-style amenities, typically $400,000–$600,000 for 4-bedroom homes.

Central Rockwall / Near The Harbor

Established neighborhoods within walking or short driving distance of The Harbor's shops and restaurants, typically $350,000–$500,000.

Getting Pre-Approved When You're Out of State

Many Rockwall buyers I work with are relocating from out of state, and buying remotely is completely normal in today's market. The process:

  1. Initial call — 15–20 minutes to discuss your income, credit range, down payment, and timeline
  2. Document upload — 2 years W-2s, 30 days paystubs, 2 months bank statements, and ID via secure link
  3. Credit pull — with your authorization, I pull a tri-merge credit report and confirm your qualifying score
  4. Pre-approval letter issued — usually within 24–48 business hours, usable with any Texas Realtor
  5. Remote offers — your Realtor handles tours and offer submission, often signed entirely via DocuSign

Out-of-state income — W-2, self-employed, or remote work — is fully acceptable. Call or text me at 469-545-7180 to start.

Property Taxes in Rockwall

Rockwall County property tax rates run approximately 2.0%–2.3% of assessed value annually. On a $460,000 home, that's roughly $9,200–$10,580/year, or about $767–$882/month added through escrow. File for the Texas Homestead Exemption at rockwallcad.com immediately after closing.
