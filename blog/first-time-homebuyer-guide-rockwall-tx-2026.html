First-Time Homebuyer Programs Rockwall TX 2026: Down Payment Help, FHA, and Rockwall County DPA

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 19, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329) and Rockwall is one of the most underrated first-time buyer opportunities in the DFW area. Lake Ray Hubbard gives the city a lifestyle draw most suburbs can't match, Rockwall ISD is consistently top-rated in the region, and — crucially — Rockwall County carries one of the highest DPA income limits in DFW. At $119,700, the Rockwall County income cap is 23% higher than Dallas or Tarrant County, which means more dual-income households qualify for TSAHC grants and TDHCA assistance here. Median prices run $360,000–$440,000, and the 2026 FHA loan limit of $563,500 covers the majority of first-time buyer inventory on the south and west sides of the city. If you've been shopping in Collin County but haven't looked at Rockwall, call me — the commute east is shorter than you think and the dollar goes further.

What first-time homebuyer programs are available in Rockwall TX in 2026?

Rockwall County buyers have access to state DPA programs with a more generous income limit than most of DFW — and I run both options on every pre-approval.

TSAHC Home Sweet Texas provides a 3–5% grant — no second lien, never repaid. On a $360,000 Rockwall purchase with FHA (3.5% down = $12,600), a 5% TSAHC grant of $17,400 fully covers the down payment with $4,800 left toward closing costs. Rockwall County income limit: $119,700 gross household (1–4 people). Minimum credit score: 620.

TDHCA My First Texas Home offers up to 5% as a deferred second loan, forgiven after 3 years in the home. Same $119,700 Rockwall County income cap. First-time buyer or veteran required. Works with FHA, conventional, or VA financing.

At $119,700, Rockwall's income limit clears the threshold for many two-income households that would be over the limit if buying in Dallas or Tarrant County. A nurse and a teacher earning $115,000 combined might not qualify for TSAHC in Garland — but they would in Rockwall.

Veterans should look at VA first — $0 down and no monthly MIP are the strongest combination when you qualify.

How much will my mortgage payment be on a home in Rockwall TX?

At a $360,000 purchase price — a realistic target for South Rockwall and the Ridge Road corridor — here's what a 3.5% down FHA loan looks like at 7.0% on a 30-year fixed:

  • Purchase price: $360,000
  • Down payment (3.5%): $12,600
  • Loan amount: $347,400
  • Principal & interest: approximately $2,312/month
  • FHA annual MIP (0.55%): approximately $159/month
  • Rockwall County property taxes (~1.6%): approximately $480/month
  • Homeowners insurance: approximately $145/month
  • Total PITI: approximately $3,096/month

Rockwall's 1.6% property tax rate is notably lower than Garland (2.0%) or Grapevine (2.2%), which is why the PITI on a Rockwall home often comes in lower than you'd expect for the price. A TSAHC 5% grant of $17,400 covers the full FHA down payment with room toward closing costs, which typically run $8,000–$11,000 on a Rockwall purchase.

Do I qualify for down payment assistance in Rockwall TX?

Rockwall County DPA income limits in 2026:

  • TSAHC Home Sweet Texas: $119,700 gross household income (Rockwall County, 1–4 people)
  • TDHCA My First Texas Home: $119,700 gross household income (Rockwall County, 1–4 people)

This $119,700 limit is one of the key reasons I point dual-income households toward Rockwall. A firefighter and a nurse earning a combined $112,000 would be over the $97,200 Dallas County limit — but they're well within Rockwall's limit. Same programs, same grant money, more households eligible. I've helped several Rockwall buyers in this exact income bracket close with DPA they couldn't have accessed in Garland or Irving.

If your score sits at 580–619, you can still use FHA at 3.5% down, but you won't qualify for TSAHC or TDHCA grant programs yet. I often run a 60–90 day credit plan with clients in that range to cross 620 — the grant money that unlocks is usually worth far more than the wait.

How do I get started buying a home in Rockwall?

  1. Pre-approval call (15 minutes): I pull soft credit, check income and assets, and confirm your Rockwall County DPA eligibility — including the higher $119,700 income limit that many dual-income households qualify under.
  2. Formal pre-approval letter (1–2 business days): South Rockwall inventory at $330K–$390K moves quickly, especially spring through fall. Your letter should be in hand before you walk into an open house.
  3. Home search (3–8 weeks): Rockwall is smaller than most DFW markets, so inventory turns over at a steady pace. I re-underwrite quickly if your target price shifts toward the Harbor or lakefront areas.
  4. Under contract to close (35–45 days): FHA with TSAHC DPA typically closes in 38–42 days. Rockwall County title companies are experienced with DPA closings — the process is straightforward.

One advantage of Rockwall few buyers realize: the lower property tax rate (1.6%) versus most DFW counties means your monthly PITI on a $360K Rockwall home is often lower than a $320K home in Dallas County with 2.2% taxes. I always run the side-by-side comparison for buyers who are deciding between markets.
