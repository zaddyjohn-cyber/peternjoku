Buying a Home in December 2026 in DFW: Why Year-End Could Be Your Best Move

Home BuyingBy Bond Peter NjokuNovember 6, 20268 min read

Most people assume the holidays are the worst time to buy a home. The conventional wisdom: inventory is low, everyone is distracted, closings pile up at year-end. I want to push back on that conventional wisdom with real DFW-specific numbers, because December 2026 has several things going for a prepared buyer that spring 2027 almost certainly will not.

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, a Garland-based mortgage loan officer, and I have helped buyers close in every month of the year. December has genuine advantages — and genuine risks. Here is an honest breakdown of both.

What are the real advantages of buying a home in DFW in December?

1. Fewer competing buyers. Active buyer traffic in DFW drops meaningfully from November through January. Families with children avoid moving during the school year. Many buyers who failed to close during peak season have mentally checked out for the year. The buyers still shopping in December are serious, and the sellers who are still listed are equally serious — often willing to negotiate terms they refused in May.

2. Motivated sellers with real deadlines. A seller who listed in September and has not sold wants to close before year-end for their own tax reasons — specifically, to avoid carrying a second set of property taxes into 2027. Sellers who accepted a job relocation are often on a forced timeline. I regularly see larger seller concessions in December than any other month because that seller motivation is highest.

3. Builder year-end inventory moves. New-construction communities in Forney, Royse City, Prosper, and Mansfield need to close specific units before December 31 to meet builder revenue targets. That urgency translates to meaningful incentives: 2-1 rate buydowns, mortgage rate caps, closing cost credits, appliance packages, and free upgrades that disappear in January when the new fiscal year starts. If you are considering new construction, November through mid-December is the ideal window to negotiate.

4. Rates are declining — third consecutive week. Freddie Mac's November 5, 2026 PMMS showed the 30-year fixed at 7.18%, the third consecutive weekly decline from a 7.40% October peak. That trend creates a window. It may not last — the December 9–10 FOMC meeting carries real uncertainty, with two voting members having already pushed for a hike at October's meeting. Locking now captures the current rate before that risk materializes.

What tax advantages does a December 2026 close create?

Texas has no state income tax, which means your federal mortgage tax picture is straightforward.

If you close in December 2026 and itemize your 2026 federal taxes:

  • Prepaid mortgage interest at closing: This covers the remaining days of December (e.g., close December 15 → 16 days of interest). On a $332,500 loan at 7.18%, that is 16 × ($332,500 × 7.18% / 365) = 16 × $65.33 = $1,045 in deductible interest in 2026.
  • Discount points paid: Points paid to buy down your rate on a purchase of a primary residence are fully deductible in the purchase year. If you paid 1 point ($3,325 on a $332,500 loan), that entire amount is deductible on your 2026 return.
  • Mortgage insurance premiums: PMI is deductible again in 2026 under the OBBBA. If you are putting less than 20% down on a conventional loan, the PMI you pay from December's closing date is deductible.

These deductions are most valuable if your total itemized deductions exceed the 2026 standard deduction ($32,200 for married filing jointly; $16,100 for single). A December close alone probably does not get you there, but combined with property tax prorations and other deductions, it can tip the scales. Talk to a tax professional about your specific situation.

What is the Texas homestead exemption timing for a December 2026 close?

This is one of the most valuable and most overlooked benefits of a December close in Texas. The homestead exemption reduces your taxable assessed value — for a typical DFW property with a $40,000 exemption, that saves roughly $600–$900 per year in property taxes depending on your county.

To qualify for the homestead exemption for the 2027 tax year, you must:

  1. Own the home as of January 1, 2027
  2. Occupy it as your primary residence on January 1, 2027
  3. File your application by April 30, 2027

A December 2026 close checks boxes 1 and 2 — you will own and occupy the home on January 1. That means you can file for the homestead exemption in January or February 2027 and have the savings reflected in your 2027 tax bill. A buyer who closes in February or March 2027 and misses the January 1 snapshot has to wait until the 2028 tax year to qualify.

What are the risks of buying in December 2026 in DFW?

I want to be honest about the challenges, too:

Scheduling compression. Title companies, appraisers, and inspectors all slow down during the holiday weeks. If your contract calls for a December 20 close and your appraiser takes vacation December 18–26, you are in trouble. Plan around the holiday calendar — the best closing windows in December are December 1–19 (before most holiday vacations begin) and December 26–30.

Lower inventory. Fewer homes are listed in December. If your criteria are very specific (particular school zone, specific floorplan), the December inventory may not have what you need, and you may be better served waiting for the February–March spring inventory surge.

FOMC risk. The December 9–10 Federal Reserve meeting could move rates up or down. Two of the ten October 28 voting members wanted to hike rates. If they carry the December meeting, mortgage rates could reverse the three-week declining trend quickly. Locking your rate before December 9 is prudent if you are under contract.

USDA-eligible buyers: December 11 risk. If you are buying in Forney, Royse City, or another USDA-eligible area with a USDA guaranteed loan, the federal government's continuing resolution expires December 11, 2026. A lapse would freeze new USDA Loan Note Guarantees. If you are under contract and using USDA, make sure your guarantee is issued before that date. Standard processing is 3–5 days after lender submission.
