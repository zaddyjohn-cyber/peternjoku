First-Time Homebuyer Programs Plano TX 2026: TSAHC Grants, FHA, and Down Payment Help

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 15, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329) and I help first-time buyers purchase homes in Plano every month. Plano ISD is consistently A-rated by the Texas Education Agency, the Legacy Business District is one of the largest employment centers in North Texas, and home values in Plano have shown long-term stability even through recent market fluctuations. Median home prices run $400,000–$480,000 — above statewide averages — but the 2026 FHA loan limit covers purchases up to $563,500, and Collin County's DPA income limit of $119,700 keeps most two-income Plano households eligible for grant assistance. Knowing which programs you qualify for before you start shopping makes all the difference in a competitive market.

What first-time homebuyer programs are available in Plano TX?

Plano sits in Collin County, which gives buyers access to two major state programs I stack together whenever possible:

TSAHC Home Sweet Texas — a grant of 3–5% of the loan amount that never has to be repaid. There's no second lien, no balloon payment, and no clawback if you sell. On a $420,000 purchase with FHA (3.5% down = $14,700), a 5% TSAHC grant of $21,000 covers the down payment in full and contributes toward closing costs. Requires a 620 credit score and income at or below $119,700 for a Collin County household of 1–4 people.

TDHCA My First Texas Home — up to 5% of the loan as a deferred second loan, with no monthly payment and forgiveness at year 3 if you remain in the home. Same income limit ($119,700 Collin County), same 620 credit score floor, and requires the buyer to be a first-time buyer or a veteran who hasn't owned a home in the past 3 years.

Both programs work with FHA, conventional, and VA loans. The right choice depends on whether you want a grant (TSAHC) or a forgivable loan that can be layered onto a conventional purchase with less than 3% rate impact (TDHCA).

FHA is the most common path for Plano first-time buyers at 580–679 FICO, especially stacked with TSAHC. Buyers at 680+ who want PMI to eventually cancel often choose conventional. Veterans and active duty almost always choose VA — it's zero down with no monthly MIP and no DPA income cap to worry about.

What will my mortgage payment be on a Plano TX home?

At Plano's median of roughly $430,000, here's a breakdown of a 5% down FHA purchase at 7.0% on a 30-year fixed:

  • Purchase price: $430,000
  • Down payment (5%): $21,500
  • Loan amount: $408,500
  • Principal & interest: approximately $2,717/month
  • FHA annual MIP (0.55%): approximately $187/month
  • Collin County property taxes (~1.5%): approximately $538/month
  • Homeowners insurance: approximately $150/month
  • Total PITI: approximately $3,592/month

With a TSAHC 5% grant ($21,500), the down payment is fully covered. You'd owe estimated closing costs of $7,000–$9,000, which seller concessions (up to 6% with FHA) can offset significantly. I've had Plano buyers close with $3,500 out of pocket by combining the grant with $8,500 in seller credits.

Do I qualify for DPA in Plano TX in 2026?

The income limit for both TSAHC and TDHCA in Collin County is $119,700 for a household of 1–4 people. That's the gross figure — no deductions. If you and your co-borrower earn a combined $85,000–$115,000, you almost certainly qualify.

Last fall I helped a Plano couple — one teacher at Plano ISD and one logistics coordinator — with a combined income of $99,500 purchasing a $418,000 townhome. Their 632 FICO qualified for FHA and for TSAHC's 5% grant, which brought their total down to $0 in down payment. They negotiated $7,200 in seller credits and closed with $4,100 out of pocket — less than most people spend on a month's rent.

What is the home buying process for first-time buyers in Plano?

  1. Pre-approval (same day): I do a soft pull, review income docs and assets, and tell you which DPA programs you qualify for in a 15-minute call.
  2. Pre-approval letter (1–2 days): Plano realtors expect a letter before showing. Offers without one get skipped in a competitive market.
  3. Home search (2–8 weeks): Plano inventory has improved from 2022 lows. Most buyers find the right home within 30–60 days.
  4. Under contract to close (30–45 days): FHA with DPA typically runs 35–45 days. I keep the process moving and your realtor informed at each step.

Plano's market rewards prepared buyers. Having your pre-approval and DPA approval in hand before you make an offer signals to sellers that you're a serious buyer — which matters when there are multiple offers on a home.
