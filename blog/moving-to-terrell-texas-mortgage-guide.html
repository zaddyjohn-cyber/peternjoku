Moving to Terrell TX — Mortgage Guide for Relocating Buyers (2026)

RelocationBy Bond Peter NjokuJuly 27, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329), and Terrell is one of the most affordable relocation destinations I work with in the eastern DFW region. It draws buyers who want small-town character, a historic downtown, and I-20 access into Dallas, all without the price tag of Forney or Rockwall. If you're moving to Terrell, this guide covers price ranges, USDA eligibility, loan programs, and how to get pre-approved before you ever set foot in Texas.

Why People Are Moving to Terrell

  • Affordability — some of the lowest home prices remaining within commuting distance of Dallas
  • I-20 corridor access — a direct commute into downtown Dallas and Mesquite
  • Historic downtown Terrell — a walkable core with local shops and older character homes
  • Broad USDA eligibility — much of the surrounding Kaufman County area qualifies for zero-down USDA financing
  • Small-town pace — a quieter, more rural feel than the fast-growing suburbs closer to Dallas
  • No state income tax — a significant draw for buyers relocating from California, Illinois, or the Northeast

Loan Programs for Terrell Buyers

USDA Loans — Zero Down for Eligible Buyers

Much of Terrell and the surrounding Kaufman County area qualifies as USDA-eligible, offering 100% financing with no down payment for income-qualified buyers. This makes USDA one of the most common loan programs I close for Terrell buyers. USDA Loan details →

FHA Loans

The 2026 FHA loan limit for Kaufman County is $563,500. FHA requires just 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score, or 10% down with a 500–579 score, and covers virtually all of Terrell's inventory. FHA Loan Terrell details →

Conventional Loans

The 2026 conforming loan limit for Kaufman County is $832,750. With 620+ credit and 3%–20% down, conventional financing is a strong option for buyers with established credit who don't need USDA's zero-down feature. Conventional Loan Terrell details →

Terrell Living at a Glance

Historic Downtown Terrell

A walkable core with local shops, restaurants, and a mix of older character homes. Typically $180,000–$260,000.

Established Residential Subdivisions

A mix of resale homes and infill new construction closer to schools and retail. Generally $240,000–$330,000.

Rural Outskirts

Larger-acreage properties, often USDA-eligible, for buyers wanting more space. Typically $300,000–$500,000+.

Getting Pre-Approved When You're Out of State

Many Terrell buyers I work with are relocating for affordability while still wanting a Dallas-area commute, and buying remotely is completely normal in today's market. The process:

  1. Initial call — 15–20 minutes to discuss your income, credit range, down payment, and timeline
  2. Document upload — 2 years W-2s, 30 days paystubs, 2 months bank statements, and ID via secure link
  3. Credit pull — with your authorization, I pull a tri-merge credit report and confirm your qualifying score
  4. Pre-approval letter issued — usually within 24–48 business hours, usable with any Texas Realtor
  5. Remote offers — your Realtor handles tours and offer submission, often signed entirely via DocuSign

Out-of-state income — W-2, self-employed, or remote work — is fully acceptable. Call or text me at 469-545-7180 to start.

Property Taxes in Terrell

Terrell (Kaufman County) property tax rates run approximately 2.0%–2.4% of assessed value annually, depending on the specific school and municipal utility district. On a $270,000 home, that's roughly $5,400–$6,480/year, or about $450–$540/month added through escrow. File for the Texas Homestead Exemption at kaufman-cad.org (Kaufman County Appraisal District) immediately after closing.
