Rockwall TX Property Taxes and Your Mortgage Payment — 2026

Property TaxesBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 14, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329), and property taxes catch more Rockwall buyers off guard than any other line item in their monthly payment. Rockwall County's combined effective tax rate runs around 1.8% of assessed value in 2026 — noticeably higher than Collin County's roughly 1.5%, which matters because it changes what house you actually qualify for, not just what you pay after closing.

What is the property tax rate in Rockwall TX for 2026?

Your total property tax bill in Rockwall is a combination of several taxing entities layered together: Rockwall County, your city (Rockwall, Heath, Fate, or Royse City), your school district (Rockwall ISD or Royse City ISD), and sometimes a municipal utility district or emergency services district. Added together, the combined effective rate lands around 1.8% of assessed value for most Rockwall addresses in 2026. I pull the exact combined rate for any specific property before I quote your payment — it does vary block to block depending on which entities apply.

How much are property taxes on a $350,000 home in Rockwall?

At the approximate 1.8% effective rate: $350,000 × 1.8% = $6,300 per year, or $525 per month. That $525/month gets escrowed directly into your mortgage payment alongside principal, interest, and insurance — your lender collects 1/12th of the annual estimate every month and pays the tax bill on your behalf when it's due.

How much does the homestead exemption save me in Rockwall?

Once you close and move in as your primary residence, you can file for a general homestead exemption, which removes $100,000 of your home's assessed value from school district taxation statewide. Rockwall's city and county may layer on additional exemptions as well. On a $350,000 home taxed at roughly 1.8%, the school-district portion of that exemption alone can save $600–$900 per year, and combined exemptions often bring total savings to $1,000–$1,800 annually. The exemption doesn't apply automatically — you have to file the paperwork with Rockwall CAD after closing, and I remind every client to do this in their first 90 days as a homeowner.

How do Rockwall property taxes affect what I qualify for?

Lenders don't just look at principal and interest when calculating your debt-to-income ratio — they include the full estimated PITI payment, taxes and insurance included. That means a higher tax rate directly lowers your qualifying loan amount, even if your income and credit score stay exactly the same. On a $350,000 Rockwall home, that $525/month tax escrow is roughly equivalent to $75,000 of extra loan principal in how it affects your DTI math. I build the actual Rockwall tax rate into every pre-approval I run, rather than using a generic statewide estimate, so the number I give you is the number that holds up at underwriting.

A recent Rockwall example

I worked with a buyer moving from Dallas to Rockwall last month — she was budgeting off a Dallas County tax estimate she'd seen online (around 2.0%) and was surprised her Rockwall payment came in lower than expected once I ran the actual combined rate. On her $365,000 home, the difference between the 2.0% estimate she'd assumed and Rockwall's actual ~1.8% rate freed up about $60/month in her qualifying budget — enough to move her into a slightly larger home in the same price search.
