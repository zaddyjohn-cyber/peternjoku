FHA Loan Denton TX 2026: Down Payment, Credit Score & Rates Explained

FHA LoansBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 3, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, and Denton is a market I've grown to know well through clients relocating for jobs at UNT, TWU, and the growing employers along the I-35 corridor. It's a college town with a genuinely diverse buyer pool — young professionals, faculty, families moving up from Lewisville and Flower Mound, and first-time buyers who assume they need a huge down payment to compete here. That's usually not true, and FHA financing is a big reason why. Here's what I walk through with every Denton client who calls or texts me at 469-545-7180.

Why FHA Is Often the Right Starting Point in Denton

FHA loans are designed for buyers who don't have 20% down saved or who have some credit history to work through. The minimum FICO score is 580 for 3.5% down, and scores between 500 and 579 can still qualify with 10% down. That's a much lower bar than conventional financing, which typically wants 620 or better — and it's why FHA remains popular with renters in Denton looking to make their first purchase.

Denton County home prices run a bit higher than Kaufman or eastern Dallas County, driven by demand near the university corridor and newer development toward Corinth and Sanger. FHA's loan limits are set higher here to reflect that, so most homes on the market are still within reach.

Stacking FHA With Down Payment Assistance

Denton County buyers who meet the roughly $119,700 income threshold common across Collin, Denton, and Rockwall counties often qualify for TSAHC Home Sweet Texas, a true grant with no repayment, or TDHCA My First Texas Home, which forgives after year three. I stack these programs with FHA financing regularly, and for a $320,000 home, the 3.5% down payment of roughly $11,200 can be substantially or fully covered depending on the program and your household size.

I run the numbers for every Denton client because eligibility and the amount available depend on your exact loan size, so I never quote a generic figure without checking your file first.

What to Expect During the FHA Process

Here's the sequence I walk Denton buyers through:

  • Pre-approval: I check credit, income, and assets and issue a pre-approval letter within about 24 hours.
  • House hunting: Your agent submits offers with my pre-approval attached — FHA offers are competitive in most Denton price ranges.
  • Appraisal and underwriting: An FHA-approved appraiser confirms the home meets minimum property standards. This matters more for Denton's older housing stock near downtown than for newer builds farther out.
  • Closing: Most FHA files I close in Denton County take 30-35 days from contract to keys.

FHA vs. Conventional for Denton Buyers

For buyers with strong credit (680+) and at least 5% down saved, conventional financing can be cheaper long-term since mortgage insurance drops off once you reach 20% equity. But for many renters and recent graduates moving into their first Denton home, FHA's lower credit threshold and DPA stacking potential usually make it the more practical path. I compare both scenarios for you before making a recommendation.
