Moving to Forney TX — Mortgage Guide for Relocating Buyers (2026)

RelocationBy Bond Peter NjokuJuly 27, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329), and Forney is one of the fastest-growing towns I work with in Kaufman County. It draws relocating families chasing new construction and an easy commute into Dallas along US-80, without the price tag of the northern DFW suburbs. If you're moving to Forney, this guide covers price ranges, loan programs including USDA eligibility, and how to get pre-approved before you ever set foot in Texas.

Why People Are Moving to Forney

  • Forney ISD — a fast-growing, well-regarded school district serving most of the town
  • US-80 corridor access — a direct, manageable commute into downtown Dallas and Mesquite
  • Abundant new construction — dozens of active subdivisions including Devonshire, Travis Ranch, and Gateway Parks
  • USDA-eligible pockets — parts of Kaufman County around Forney qualify for zero-down USDA financing
  • Lower cost of entry — new construction at prices well below Rockwall or Collin County
  • No state income tax — a significant draw for buyers relocating from California, Illinois, or the Northeast

Loan Programs for Forney Buyers

Conventional (Most Common in Forney)

The 2026 conforming loan limit for Kaufman County is $832,750. With 620+ credit and 3%–20% down, conventional financing covers virtually every Forney purchase, and I regularly time closings to builder draw schedules. Conventional Loan Forney details →

USDA Loans — Zero Down in Eligible Areas

Parts of Kaufman County around Forney qualify as USDA-eligible, offering 100% financing with no down payment for income-qualified buyers. I check eligibility address by address since USDA maps can change block by block. USDA Loan details →

FHA Loans

The 2026 FHA loan limit for Kaufman County is $563,500. FHA requires just 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score, or 10% down with a 500–579 score, and covers most of Forney's new-construction inventory. FHA Loan Forney details →

Forney Neighborhoods at a Glance

Devonshire

A large master-planned community with a resort-style amenity center and a wide range of floor plans. Homes typically range from $330,000–$450,000.

Travis Ranch

An established community with a mix of home ages and price points, popular with first-time buyers. Generally $260,000–$350,000.

Gateway Parks

Newer premium construction with larger lots on Forney's growing edge. Typically $400,000–$550,000.

Getting Pre-Approved When You're Out of State

Many Forney buyers I work with are relocating for a Dallas-area job and want new construction without the higher price tags to the north, and buying remotely is completely normal in today's market. The process:

  1. Initial call — 15–20 minutes to discuss your income, credit range, down payment, and timeline
  2. Document upload — 2 years W-2s, 30 days paystubs, 2 months bank statements, and ID via secure link
  3. Credit pull — with your authorization, I pull a tri-merge credit report and confirm your qualifying score
  4. Pre-approval letter issued — usually within 24–48 business hours, usable with any Texas Realtor or builder
  5. Remote offers or builder contracts — your Realtor handles tours, or I coordinate directly with the builder's preferred process

Out-of-state income — W-2, self-employed, or remote work — is fully acceptable. Call or text me at 469-545-7180 to start.

Property Taxes in Forney

Forney (Kaufman County) property tax rates run approximately 2.2%–2.6% of assessed value annually, depending on the specific school and municipal utility district. On a $350,000 home, that's roughly $7,700–$9,100/year, or about $642–$758/month added through escrow. File for the Texas Homestead Exemption at kaufman-cad.org (Kaufman County Appraisal District) immediately after closing.
