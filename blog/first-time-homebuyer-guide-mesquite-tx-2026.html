First-Time Homebuyer Programs Mesquite TX 2026: Down Payment Help, FHA, and Dallas County DPA

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 18, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329) and Mesquite is one of the strongest first-time buyer markets in the entire DFW metro. Median home prices sit around $240,000–$270,000 — some of the lowest in Dallas County — while the 2026 FHA loan limit is $563,500, meaning virtually every home in the city is FHA-eligible. Dallas County's income limit for down payment assistance is $97,200, and most Mesquite households earning a single income qualify with room to spare. Mesquite Independent School District is consistently rated among the better large districts in North Texas, the town has convenient I-20 and US-80 access to both Dallas and Garland, and home inventory stays healthier here than in many surrounding suburbs. If you've been told you can't afford to buy yet, Mesquite is often where I tell buyers to start looking.

What first-time homebuyer programs are available in Mesquite TX in 2026?

Mesquite buyers in Dallas County have access to the two primary state DPA programs, and I run both to find the combination that minimizes what you bring to the table at closing.

TSAHC Home Sweet Texas is a 3–5% grant of your loan amount — no second lien, no repayment, ever. On a $235,000 Mesquite purchase with FHA (3.5% down = $8,225), a 5% TSAHC grant of $11,300 covers the entire down payment with room toward closing costs. Income limit: $97,200 gross household (Dallas County, 1–4 people). Minimum credit score: 620.

TDHCA My First Texas Home offers up to 5% as a deferred second loan, forgiven at year 3. Same $97,200 income cap. First-time buyer required (or veteran). Pairs with FHA at Mesquite's price points and can cover both down payment and closing costs depending on loan amount.

Mesquite's lower median price makes the math particularly favorable: on a $235,000–$255,000 purchase, a 5% TSAHC grant often exceeds the FHA down payment entirely, leaving the full surplus toward closing costs. Many Mesquite first-time buyers I've worked with close with less than $1,500 out of pocket.

How much will my mortgage payment be on a home in Mesquite TX?

At a $235,000 purchase price — just under Mesquite's median, targeting a move-in ready home — here's what a 3.5% down FHA loan looks like at 7.0% on a 30-year fixed:

  • Purchase price: $235,000
  • Down payment (3.5%): $8,225
  • Loan amount: $226,775
  • Principal & interest: approximately $1,509/month
  • FHA annual MIP (0.55%): approximately $104/month
  • Dallas County property taxes (~2.0%): approximately $392/month
  • Homeowners insurance: approximately $110/month
  • Total PITI: approximately $2,115/month

Apply a TSAHC 5% grant ($11,300) and your $8,225 down payment disappears — and there's still $3,075 toward closing costs, which typically run $5,500–$8,000 on a Mesquite purchase. Ask the seller for a $5,000 closing cost contribution and you might close for next to nothing out of pocket.

Do I qualify for down payment assistance in Mesquite TX?

Dallas County DPA income limits in 2026:

  • TSAHC Home Sweet Texas: $97,200 gross household income (1–4 people)
  • TDHCA My First Texas Home: $97,200 gross household income (1–4 people)

Mesquite's income profile is a strong fit. Most households I work with in Mesquite earn $45,000–$80,000 — single teachers, logistics workers, healthcare aides, and young families on one income. At Mesquite's price points, FHA monthly payments often run $200–$400 less per month than comparable market-rate rentals in the same neighborhoods, which is the argument I make to renters who think they can't afford to buy.

A recent client in East Mesquite — single income of $58,000, 625 FICO, renting for $1,650/month — purchased a $249,000 home using FHA with a TSAHC 5% grant. Total cash to close was $890. New PITI: $2,200/month. She's building equity instead of paying rent, and I got her pre-approved in the same week she called.

How do I get started buying a home in Mesquite?

  1. Pre-approval call (15 minutes): I pull soft credit, check income and assets, and confirm your DPA eligibility that same call. For Mesquite buyers, this usually takes a single 15-minute conversation.
  2. Formal pre-approval letter (1–2 business days): Move-in-ready homes in Scyene and East Mesquite under $250K see solid competition. Your letter is what gets your offer taken seriously.
  3. Home search (2–5 weeks): Mesquite has healthy inventory compared to Collin County suburbs. I re-underwrite quickly if your price point shifts.
  4. Under contract to close (35–45 days): FHA with DPA typically closes in 35–42 days from signed contract. I coordinate with title directly and give your realtor weekly milestone updates.

The most common mistake I see Mesquite first-time buyers make is staying in a lease because they assume they don't have enough saved. In most cases, a TSAHC grant plus seller concessions means they needed less in savings than they were paying in last month's rent deposit.
