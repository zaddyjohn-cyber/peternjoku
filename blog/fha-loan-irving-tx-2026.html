I'm Bond Peter Njoku, and Irving is one of the more overlooked markets I work in — sandwiched between Dallas and Grapevine, close to DFW Airport and the Las Colinas business district, with a mix of established neighborhoods and newer condos that fit squarely in FHA's price range. Why FHA Works Well in Irving

Irving's housing stock ranges from starter homes near Northwest Highway to newer townhomes and condos near Las Colinas, most of which fall well within FHA's Dallas County loan limits. FHA's lower credit threshold (580 versus 620+ for most conventional loans) and its 3.5% down payment make it the go-to option for renters making the jump to homeownership in Irving, especially given the city's proximity to DFW Airport jobs and the growing Las Colinas corporate corridor.

FHA Loan Limits for Irving, TX in 2026

Irving falls within Dallas County, which uses the standard Dallas-Fort Worth metro FHA loan limit. This limit comfortably covers the vast majority of Irving's single-family and condo listings. If you're looking at a higher-priced property near Las Colinas, I'll confirm whether it fits under the current limit before you write an offer.

Stacking Down Payment Assistance With FHA in Irving

Because FHA only requires 3.5% down, it pairs extremely well with down payment assistance programs like TSAHC's Home Sweet Texas grant or TDHCA's My First Texas Home. For many Irving buyers I work with, that combination means the down payment is fully covered by grant funds, leaving only closing costs to plan for.

What to Expect From the FHA Process in Irving

FHA loans require an FHA-approved appraisal, which checks for both value and basic safety/livability standards — something to be aware of with Irving's older housing stock near the city center. I walk every client through what the appraiser will flag before the inspection happens, so there are no surprises mid-contract.
