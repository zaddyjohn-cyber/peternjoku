First-Time Homebuyer Programs Lewisville TX 2026: TSAHC, TDHCA, FHA, and Conventional

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 16, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329) and I help first-time buyers close on homes in Lewisville every month. Lewisville sits on Denton County's Lake Lewisville shoreline, offering a lake lifestyle within easy reach of both Dallas and Fort Worth, with median home prices running $310,000–$390,000. As part of Denton County, Lewisville buyers qualify for a $119,700 down payment assistance income limit — one of the more generous thresholds in the DFW area. Here's what first-time buyers in Lewisville need to know for 2026.

What first-time homebuyer programs are available in Lewisville TX in 2026?

Lewisville buyers have four main paths to homeownership, and I check every client against all of them:

TSAHC Home Sweet Texas — a grant of 3–5% of the loan amount, never repaid. Available at Denton County's $119,700 income limit for a 1–4 person household. Requires 620 FICO minimum. No first-time buyer requirement.

TDHCA My First Texas Home — up to 5% deferred, forgiven at year 3. Same $119,700 Denton County income cap. Requires first-time buyer status or veteran re-entry.

FHA loans — 3.5% down at 580 FICO, or 10% down at scores as low as 500. Stacks with either DPA program above.

Conventional loans — as little as 3% down for qualified first-time buyers with 620+ FICO, with PMI that drops off once you hit 80% loan-to-value.

What will my mortgage payment be on a Lewisville TX home?

At Lewisville's median of roughly $350,000, here's a 3.5% down FHA purchase at 7.0% on a 30-year fixed:

  • Purchase price: $350,000
  • Down payment (3.5%): $12,250
  • Loan amount: $337,750
  • Principal & interest: approximately $2,247/month
  • FHA annual MIP (0.55%): approximately $155/month
  • Denton County property taxes (~1.8%): approximately $525/month
  • Homeowners insurance: approximately $145/month
  • Total PITI: approximately $3,072/month

With a TSAHC 5% grant ($17,500), the down payment is fully covered with money remaining for closing costs. Closing costs on a $350,000 Lewisville purchase typically run $6,000–$8,500, and FHA allows sellers to contribute up to 6% ($21,000) toward those costs.

Does buying near Lake Lewisville affect my insurance or financing?

Homes near Lake Lewisville itself may sit in or near a flood zone, which can require flood insurance in addition to standard homeowners coverage — I always check FEMA flood maps before locking in a payment estimate for lakefront or near-lake listings. Homes elsewhere in the city carry standard insurance costs. Beyond that, FHA, conventional, and DPA programs all apply the same way regardless of proximity to the lake, so financing terms don't change — only your total housing cost might, depending on flood zone designation.

Earlier this year I helped a family relocating to Lewisville for work near the DFW Airport corridor — combined income of $94,000, 660 FICO, targeting a $360,000 home a few miles from the lake. Their income qualified comfortably under Denton County's $119,700 limit, and we used TSAHC's 5% grant ($18,000) to cover their down payment on an FHA loan. They closed with $2,400 out of pocket after a seller credit covered most remaining closing costs.
