Down Payment Assistance in Denton, TX (2026 Guide)

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuJuly 29, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, a licensed Mortgage Loan Officer (NMLS #2670329), and Denton's mix of university-area renters, young professionals, and growing families makes it one of the DFW markets where down payment assistance gets the most use. Denton sits in Denton County, where the income ceiling on TSAHC and TDHCA assistance is set higher than in Dallas or Tarrant County — good news for buyers just starting out.

Why Denton County's Income Limits Work in Your Favor

Because Denton is in Denton County, the 2026 income limit for TSAHC and TDHCA assistance sits around $119,700 for a smaller household, compared to roughly $97,200 in Dallas and Tarrant counties. That gap matters for Denton's younger buyers and dual-income households who might assume they're priced out of assistance based on income alone.

Denton's median home price generally runs $350,000–$500,000, so a 5% grant of $17,500–$25,000 makes a meaningful dent in upfront costs. I most often pair this with FHA financing for Denton's first-time buyers, and with conventional financing when a buyer's credit and income support skipping FHA's mortgage insurance premium.

A Realistic Denton Scenario

On a $375,000 Denton home, a 5% Home Sweet Texas grant is $18,750. Paired with FHA's 3.5% down requirement of about $13,125, that leaves $5,625 extra toward closing costs or a rate buydown. For buyers weighing FHA against conventional, I run the full comparison — including long-term mortgage insurance costs — before we settle on a structure.

What down payment assistance is available for Denton TX buyers in 2026?

Denton buyers can use TSAHC Home Sweet Texas (a 3-5% grant with no repayment) and TDHCA My First Texas Home (up to 5%, forgiven at year three), both statewide programs I place regularly for Denton County clients.
