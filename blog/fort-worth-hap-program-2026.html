Fort Worth HAP Program 2026 Explained

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuJuly 30, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, a licensed Mortgage Loan Officer (NMLS #2670329), and Fort Worth's Homebuyer Assistance Program (HAP) is one of the more generous city-run down payment programs I work with anywhere in DFW. It can provide up to $25,000 toward a down payment and closing costs for eligible first-time buyers, and because it's forgivable rather than a straight loan, most of my Fort Worth clients never repay a dollar of it as long as they stay put for the program's affordability period.

How Fort Worth HAP Assistance Works

HAP is administered by the City of Fort Worth and funds up to $25,000 in assistance, structured as a forgivable loan tied to a 5-year affordability period. As long as you keep the home as your primary residence through that period, the balance is forgiven — you don't make payments on it and you don't owe it back at closing on a future sale. If you sell or refinance out of the program early, a prorated portion may come due, which is one of the first things I walk every Fort Worth buyer through before we apply.

Pairing HAP With FHA Financing

I pair Fort Worth HAP with an FHA loan for the vast majority of my Fort Worth clients, since FHA's 3.5% minimum down payment is easily covered by the program's assistance amount — often with money left over for closing costs. On a $300,000 Fort Worth home, FHA's minimum down payment is $10,500, meaning a full $25,000 HAP allocation covers that down payment more than twice over, freeing up the rest for rate buydowns, prepaid taxes and insurance, or lender fees. Some qualified buyers also use it alongside a conventional loan if their credit profile supports it.
