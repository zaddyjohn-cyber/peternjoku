First-Time Homebuyer Programs Arlington TX 2026: Down Payment Help, FHA, and Tarrant County DPA

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 20, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329) and Arlington is one of the most affordable entry points for first-time homebuyers in the entire DFW metro. Median home prices hover around $270,000–$310,000 — well below what buyers pay for similar square footage in Plano, Frisco, or even Fort Worth's newer suburbs. The 2026 FHA loan limit of $563,500 covers virtually every home in Arlington, and Tarrant County's $97,200 DPA income limit keeps grant programs within reach of most working households. Arlington has a diverse economy built around manufacturing, entertainment, and UTA — a 90,000-student university that makes the city younger and more renter-heavy than surrounding suburbs, which means there's strong demand from people ready to convert from renting to owning. If you're in that group, call me before your next lease renewal.

What first-time homebuyer programs are available in Arlington TX in 2026?

Arlington buyers in Tarrant County have access to two strong state DPA programs, and I stack them strategically to minimize cash at closing.

TSAHC Home Sweet Texas provides a 3–5% grant — real grant money, no repayment ever. On a $265,000 Arlington purchase with FHA (3.5% down = $9,275), a 5% TSAHC grant of $12,800 fully covers the down payment with $3,525 left toward closing costs. Income limit: $97,200 gross household (Tarrant County, 1–4 people). Minimum credit score: 620.

TDHCA My First Texas Home offers up to 5% as a deferred second loan, forgiven after 3 years in the home. Same $97,200 Tarrant County income cap. First-time buyer or veteran required. Pairs with FHA or conventional financing.

Note: Fort Worth HAP (up to $25,000 forgivable) applies to properties inside Fort Worth city limits — most Arlington addresses don't qualify. TSAHC is the primary workhorse for Arlington first-time buyers, and it's a strong one at Arlington's price points.

How much will my mortgage payment be on a home in Arlington TX?

At a $265,000 purchase price — typical for South Arlington and Southeast Arlington starter homes — here's what a 3.5% down FHA loan looks like at 7.0% on a 30-year fixed:

  • Purchase price: $265,000
  • Down payment (3.5%): $9,275
  • Loan amount: $255,725
  • Principal & interest: approximately $1,703/month
  • FHA annual MIP (0.55%): approximately $117/month
  • Tarrant County property taxes (~2.0%): approximately $442/month
  • Homeowners insurance: approximately $120/month
  • Total PITI: approximately $2,382/month

A 5% TSAHC grant of $12,800 covers the $9,275 down payment completely, with $3,525 left over. Closing costs on a $265K purchase typically run $6,000–$8,500. Ask the seller to contribute $5,000–$6,000 toward closing costs and many Arlington first-time buyers close under $1,000 out of pocket.

Do I qualify for down payment assistance in Arlington TX?

Tarrant County DPA income limits in 2026:

  • TSAHC Home Sweet Texas: $97,200 gross household income (Tarrant County, 1–4 people)
  • TDHCA My First Texas Home: $97,200 gross household income (Tarrant County, 1–4 people)

Arlington's income profile maps well to the $97,200 threshold — many households here earn $55,000–$90,000. UTA graduates early in their careers, hospital workers, and manufacturing and logistics employees are common buyers in my Arlington pipeline. A recent client — UTA grad, first job at $64,000, 638 FICO — purchased a $259,000 home in Southeast Arlington using FHA plus a TSAHC 5% grant. He brought $850 to closing after seller concessions. Monthly PITI: $2,290. Previous rent: $1,650. Net difference with equity building: worth it for him.

How do I get started buying a home in Arlington?

  1. Pre-approval call (15 minutes): I pull soft credit, check income and assets, and run your DPA eligibility on the same call. Most Arlington buyers know their loan options within 15 minutes of calling me.
  2. Formal pre-approval letter (1–2 business days): South Arlington starter homes under $270K see strong interest. Your letter is what separates your offer from the other three on the table.
  3. Home search (2–6 weeks): Arlington's large inventory means you usually find something in range within a month. I stay available to re-underwrite if your target price shifts.
  4. Under contract to close (35–45 days): FHA with TSAHC DPA closes in 35–42 days on average. I manage all DPA documentation in parallel with underwriting so there are no late surprises.

The most common mistake Arlington first-time buyers make is comparing their current rent to a mortgage payment without factoring in that rent grows every year. On a fixed-rate mortgage, your principal and interest payment is locked for 30 years. I run that 10-year side-by-side comparison for every buyer who asks — and it's almost always the clearest argument for buying sooner.
