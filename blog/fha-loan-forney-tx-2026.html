FHA Loan Forney TX 2026: Down Payment, Credit Score & Rates Explained

FHA LoansBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 3, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, and I spend a lot of my week on the phone with buyers moving into Forney. It's one of the fastest-growing towns in Kaufman County, and it's also one of the most affordable entry points into the greater Dallas market right now — which is exactly why FHA loans are such a good fit here. If you're weighing whether an FHA loan makes sense for a Forney purchase in 2026, here's what I tell every client who calls or texts me at 469-545-7180 asking the same question.

Why FHA Works So Well for Forney Buyers

FHA loans exist to help buyers who don't have a large down payment saved or who have had some bumps in their credit history. Forney's median home price is still well below Dallas proper and Collin County, which means the FHA loan limit for Kaufman County comfortably covers most homes for sale here. I've closed FHA loans for teachers, warehouse workers, first responders, and young families relocating from Dallas and Mesquite looking for more space and lower property taxes — Forney checks a lot of those boxes.

The core FHA requirements haven't changed much heading into 2026: a minimum 580 FICO score gets you 3.5% down, and if your score sits between 500 and 579, you can still qualify with 10% down. Compare that to conventional loans, which typically want 620 or higher, and you can see why FHA remains the go-to option for so many first-time buyers in this market.

Stacking FHA With Down Payment Assistance in Forney

This is where I add the most value for my Forney clients. FHA's 3.5% down requirement is already low, but it doesn't have to come out of your own pocket at all. I regularly stack FHA loans with down payment assistance programs like TSAHC Home Sweet Texas (a true grant, no repayment) or TDHCA My First Texas Home, which is forgiven after year three. For a $280,000 home, 3.5% down is roughly $9,800 — a DPA grant can wipe that number down to just your closing costs, or even cover a portion of those too.

Kaufman County's income limits for these programs sit around the same range as neighboring Dallas and Rockwall counties, so most Forney households working full-time jobs in the DFW metro qualify. I walk through the math with every client before we submit an application, because the combination changes based on your exact loan amount and household size.

What the FHA Process Looks Like Step by Step

Buyers moving from renting to owning in Forney usually ask me what actually happens after they call. Here's the short version:

  • Pre-approval: I pull your credit, verify income and assets, and give you a real number to shop with — usually within 24 hours.
  • House hunting: Your real estate agent submits offers with my pre-approval letter attached. FHA loans are treated the same as any other financing by most sellers in this market.
  • Appraisal and underwriting: FHA requires an FHA-approved appraiser to confirm the home meets minimum property standards — this is stricter than a conventional appraisal but rarely a dealbreaker on newer Forney construction.
  • Closing: Most of my FHA files in Kaufman County close in 30-35 days from contract to keys.

FHA vs. Conventional vs. USDA for Forney Addresses

Because parts of Forney sit in USDA-eligible territory, I always check that first for qualifying buyers — zero down beats 3.5% down every time when you qualify. If USDA's income limits or property location don't work for your situation, FHA is almost always my next recommendation over conventional financing for anyone with a credit score under 680, simply because the qualifying guidelines are more forgiving. I run all three scenarios side by side before recommending anything, and it costs you nothing to have me do that math.
