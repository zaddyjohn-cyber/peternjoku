Down Payment Assistance in Forney, TX (2026 Guide)

First-Time BuyersBy Bond Peter NjokuAugust 9, 20268 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku, a licensed Mortgage Loan Officer (NMLS #2670329), and Forney is one of the fastest-growing markets I work in — new subdivisions going up constantly, and a lot of first-time buyers moving out from Dallas and Mesquite for more house at a lower price. What most of them don't realize until we talk is that Forney also sits largely inside a USDA-eligible zone, which changes the down payment conversation entirely.

Kaufman County's Income Limits

Forney sits in Kaufman County, where the 2026 income limit for TSAHC and TDHCA assistance is approximately $97,200 for a one-to-two person household — closer to Dallas County's limit than to neighboring Rockwall County's higher $119,700. For USDA specifically, Kaufman County's limit runs higher: around $112,450 for a household of 1-4 and $148,450 for a household of 5-8. I check both limits since they apply to different programs.

A Realistic Forney Scenario

On a $280,000 Forney home, a 5% Home Sweet Texas grant is $14,000, which paired with FHA's 3.5% down of $9,800 leaves about $4,200 for closing costs. If that same property falls in a USDA-eligible zone — a very likely scenario in Forney — a buyer could close with no down payment at all. I run both scenarios so you see the real difference before we pick a strategy.

What down payment assistance is available for Forney TX buyers in 2026?

Forney buyers can use TSAHC Home Sweet Texas (a 3-5% grant with no repayment) and TDHCA My First Texas Home (up to 5%, forgiven at year three). In much of Forney, yes — USDA Rural Development loans offer 100% financing with no down payment at all, which is often a stronger option than a grant-plus-FHA structure since Forney is one of the larger USDA-eligible pockets in the DFW area. Not for every program. TSAHC Home Sweet Texas has no first-time buyer requirement, so a Forney move-up buyer can use it too. TDHCA My First Texas Home requires first-time buyer status unless you're a veteran, and USDA has no first-time buyer requirement at all.
