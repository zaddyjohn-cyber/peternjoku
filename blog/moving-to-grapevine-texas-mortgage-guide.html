Moving to Grapevine TX — Mortgage Guide for Relocating Buyers (2026)

RelocationBy Bond Peter NjokuJuly 26, 20269 min read

I'm Bond Peter Njoku (NMLS #2670329), and Grapevine is one of the most charming small cities I work in across North Texas. It draws relocating buyers who want historic small-town character, wineries, and lake access, all while sitting directly next to DFW International Airport. If you're moving to Grapevine, this guide covers price ranges, neighborhoods, loan programs, and how to get pre-approved before you ever set foot in Texas.

Why People Are Moving to Grapevine

  • Historic Main Street — a walkable downtown district with shops, restaurants, and the annual GrapeFest celebration
  • Texas wineries — Grapevine calls itself the "Christmas Capital of Texas" and is also home to a growing wine trail with tasting rooms downtown
  • DFW International Airport access — bordering the airport, ideal for frequent travelers and airline or logistics employees
  • Grapevine-Colleyville ISD — a highly rated school district serving the city
  • Lake Grapevine — parks, marinas, and trails for boating and outdoor recreation
  • No state income tax — a major draw for buyers relocating from California, Illinois, or the Northeast

Loan Programs for Grapevine Buyers

Conventional (Most Common in Grapevine)

The 2026 conforming loan limit for Tarrant County is $806,500. With 620+ credit and 3%–20% down, conventional is the most flexible option across Grapevine's price range. PMI is required with less than 20% down but cancels automatically at 80% loan-to-value. Conventional Loan Grapevine details →

FHA Loans

The 2026 FHA loan limit for Tarrant County is $524,225. FHA requires just 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score, or 10% down with a 500–579 score, and covers much of Grapevine's entry and mid-range resale market. FHA Loan Grapevine details →

VA Loans — Zero Down for Veterans

Veterans with full entitlement can purchase anywhere in Grapevine with zero down payment and no loan limit. I'm experienced with VA loans in Tarrant County and can walk you through the COE process and funding fee structure. VA Loan details →

Grapevine Neighborhoods at a Glance

Historic Downtown / Main Street

Walkable neighborhoods with a mix of original character homes and updated properties close to shops and restaurants. Typically $400,000–$600,000.

Near Lake Grapevine

Newer construction and updated homes with quick access to the lake's marinas and trails. Generally $550,000–$800,000.

Heritage / South Grapevine

Established residential areas with mature trees and larger lots. A mix of original and renovated homes, typically $350,000–$500,000.

Getting Pre-Approved When You're Out of State

Many Grapevine buyers I work with are relocating for airline, logistics, or corporate roles near DFW Airport, and buying remotely is completely normal in today's market. The process:

  1. Initial call — 15–20 minutes to discuss your income, credit range, down payment, and timeline
  2. Document upload — 2 years W-2s, 30 days paystubs, 2 months bank statements, and ID via secure link
  3. Credit pull — with your authorization, I pull a tri-merge credit report and confirm your qualifying score
  4. Pre-approval letter issued — usually within 24–48 business hours, usable with any Texas Realtor
  5. Remote offers — your Realtor handles tours and offer submission, often signed entirely via DocuSign

Out-of-state income — W-2, self-employed, or remote work — is fully acceptable. Call or text me at 469-545-7180 to start.

Property Taxes in Grapevine

Grapevine (Tarrant County) property tax rates run approximately 2.0%–2.4% of assessed value annually. On a $500,000 home, that's roughly $10,000–$12,000/year, or about $833–$1,000/month added through escrow. File for the Texas Homestead Exemption at tad.org (Tarrant Appraisal District) immediately after closing.
