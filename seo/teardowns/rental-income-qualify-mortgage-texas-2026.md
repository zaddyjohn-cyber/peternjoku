## Data Points
- Fannie Mae SEL-2026-08: published Sept 2, 2026, mandatory for applications Nov 1, 2026+
- 75% rule: $2,000 market rent × 75% = $1,500 qualifying income
- 2-year Schedule E for existing properties
- New 45-day rule: property bought within 45 days → lender uses appraisal rent schedule, not lease
- FHA multi-unit: can use 75% of projected rents with no rental history
- DFW typical 2BR rents: Garland $1,300–$1,500, Mesquite $1,200–$1,400, McKinney $1,800–$2,000, Frisco $2,000–$2,200
- DFW duplex prices: Garland/Mesquite area $250K–$320K
- Conforming limit: $832,750
