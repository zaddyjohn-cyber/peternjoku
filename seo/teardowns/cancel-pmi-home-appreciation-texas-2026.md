FHFA Sept 15, 2026: Fannie Mae aligned with Freddie Mac to allow servicers to PROACTIVELY contact borrowers who may qualify to cancel PMI. Key rules:
- Automatic termination: when loan reaches 78% LTV on original amortization schedule
- Request cancellation: at 80% LTV (original value), 24-month minimum seasoning
- Appreciation-based: 80% LTV (2+ years), 75% LTV (2–5 years), new appraisal required
