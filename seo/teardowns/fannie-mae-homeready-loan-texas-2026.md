## ⚠️ DATA FLAGS

**Dallas HomeReady income limit discrepancy:**
- mortgage-info.com competitor says Dallas, TX limit = $83,200/year (4-person? unclear)
- homebuyingbrief.com says Dallas County 2026 AMI = $121,100 (4-person) → 80% = $96,900
- CLAUDE.md DPA income limit for Dallas/Tarrant = $97,200 (close to $96,900)
- **Use $96,900 as the 4-person Dallas County figure** (80% of $121,100 HUD AMI). The $83,200 appears to be the 3-person or 2-person figure or an outdated number. Do not use $83,200 without verification.
- Note: Fannie Mae's AMI lookup at fanniemae.com is authoritative — flag that buyers should verify exact limit at the Fannie AMI tool at origination.
