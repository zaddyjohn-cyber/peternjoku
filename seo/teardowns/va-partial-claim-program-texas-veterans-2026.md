## Key Verified Facts for the Post

- Program opened: June 15, 2026 (VA press release)
- Law authorizing it: part of VA's new loss-mitigation authority
- How it works: servicer advances missed payments → becomes subordinate lien with NO INTEREST, NO MONTHLY PAYMENT
- Repaid when: sold, refinanced, loan paid off
- Servicer deadline to fully implement: November 28, 2026
- Three-month trial payment plan: required before claim can be submitted
- Eligible: veterans in default, experiencing financial hardship, who can sustain current payment going forward
- DFW context: Dallas County active foreclosures up 24% YoY (4,755 active from our existing post)
- Related programs: VASP (ended earlier 2026, replaced by partial claim), forbearance, loss mitigation
