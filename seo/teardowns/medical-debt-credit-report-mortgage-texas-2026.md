## ⚠️ DATA FLAG — verify before publishing
Confirm the court vacatur fact independently before publishing (done — two independent searches
agree): the CFPB's medical-debt credit-reporting ban was vacated by a federal court in the **Eastern
District of Texas on July 11, 2025**, on the grounds CFPB exceeded its Fair Credit Reporting Act
authority. As of 2026 the federal ban is NOT in effect. Texas is NOT among the ~15 states with their
own medical-debt credit-reporting restriction laws (CA, CO, CT, DE, IL, ME, MD, MN, NV, NY, OR, RI,
VT, VA, WA are the confirmed list) — so Texas buyers rely solely on: (a) the three bureaus' 2023
voluntary policy (paid medical collections removed immediately; unpaid debt gets a 12-month grace
period before appearing; collections under $500 excluded entirely) and (b) GSE automated
underwriting systems that already de-weight medical collections in scoring since a 2023 update.
