# Teardown: "2026 conforming loan limit Texas / DFW"

## Verified facts (WebFetch/WebSearch, 2026-09-19 — FHFA official release confirmed)
- FHFA announced Nov 25, 2025: 2026 baseline one-unit conforming loan limit = **$832,750**
  (fhfa.gov/news/news-release/fhfa-announces-conforming-loan-limit-values-for-2026)
- Up $26,250 from 2025's $806,500 baseline
- Reflects 3.26% average home-price growth, Q3 2024 to Q3 2025 (FHFA House Price Index)
- No Texas county exceeds the baseline in 2026 (all DFW counties use $832,750, same as FHA's shared
  high-cost figure of $563,500 across the DFW MSA)
