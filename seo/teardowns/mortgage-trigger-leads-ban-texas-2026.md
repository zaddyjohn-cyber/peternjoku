## Weaknesses to exploit
- Most of the SERP (bankrate, WBTV, bank blogs) is **pre-law** and still describes trigger leads as legal and unlimited.
- NAMB is written for brokers, not for the borrower whose phone is ringing.
- Nobody has a before/after table, a "why am I STILL getting calls" section, or a scam-call checklist.
- No Texas/DFW buyer context; no rate-shopping inquiry-window guidance.
