# Teardown: "government shutdown USDA loan Texas 2026"

## Verified facts (WebSearch, 2026-09-18)
- Congress passed a stopgap CR; Trump signed it Sept 2, 2026, funding government at FY2026 levels through **December 11, 2026** — averted the Oct 1 shutdown threat. House vote 370-48; Senate 90-6 in August. Congress returns to this fight in the lame-duck session after the Nov 3 midterms.
- ⚠️ Do not claim the CR itself named USDA — the CR is a general funding bill. USDA's shutdown-contingency behavior is separate, standing agency policy, confirmed via general shutdown-impact reporting (mpamag.com, Oct 2025 shutdown coverage; usdaloans.com).
- What actually freezes: USDA Rural Development cannot issue **final loan commitments / Loan Note Guarantees** during a lapse — unlike FHA/VA, which lenders can underwrite and close independently of the agency during a shutdown. Conventional (Fannie/Freddie) is essentially unaffected since the GSEs aren't funded through annual appropriations.
- Real-world effects from the most recent (2025) shutdown: delayed closings, rate locks expiring before closing (forcing costly extensions), some sellers hesitant to accept USDA-financed offers due to funding uncertainty, and a backlog once government reopens.
