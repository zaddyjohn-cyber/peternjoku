# Teardown — "does USDA loan have maximum purchase price limit Texas 2026"

Source: sept-2026-page1-plan.md Tier A #8. Google search run 2026-09-12.

## SERP
- fairway.com/griffinfunding/madisonmortgageguys/usdaloans.net/usdaloans.com/mrrate.com — all national
  explainer sites, no Texas/DFW-specific page ranking. Weak SERP, winnable on relevance.
- Consensus across pages: USDA **Guaranteed** loans (the common type Peter offers) have **no
  maximum purchase price** — only income and debt-to-income limits. Only the rarer **Direct** loan
  program (income-restricted, USDA-serviced, not broker-originated) has a regional price cap.
- Several pages conflate this with income limits and quote the stale national floor
  ($119,850/$158,250) instead of the metro-specific figure.

## ⚠️ DATA FLAG
Competitor pages cite the **national floor** income limit ($119,850 / $158,250). Per CLAUDE.md,
the verified DFW HUD Metro FMR Area guaranteed-loan limit (Kaufman, Rockwall, Ellis, Dallas) is
**$139,300 (1–4 person) / $183,900 (5–8 person)**, confirmed 2026-09-03 directly from
eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov. Use the DFW figure, not the floor competitors quote.
