  page. Two local-ish results: chancemcgheelaw.com and ackerwarren.com and filebankruptcyhouston.com —
  all bankruptcy ATTORNEY sites, not lender sites, wrong intent/CTA (they want a bankruptcy client,
  not a mortgage client). This SERP has zero real lender competition — winnable.

## Waiting periods (cross-checked, use these)
- FHA: 2 years from Chapter 7 discharge (standard); some lenders allow "extenuating circumstances"
  exception at 1 year with documented compensating factors.
- VA: 2 years from discharge.
- Conventional (Fannie/Freddie): 4 years from discharge (2 years only under documented extenuating
  circumstances).
- USDA: 3 years from discharge.
