## Differentiation strategy
Nitin Gupta is a buyer's agent, not a licensed loan officer — his content skews toward negotiation/
agent-side advice. Bond Peter's angle should be the financing/qualification mechanics a real estate
agent can't speak to with authority: how ARM qualification (DTI at the fully-indexed rate, not the

fixed loan.

## Verified facts (cross-checked)
- As of Sept 21, 2026: 30yr fixed TX ~7.25%, 15yr fixed ~6.5%, 7/1 ARM ~6.625% (one source); Dallas
  30yr purchase rate ~6.75-7.37% depending on the specific survey/date within the week.
- August 2026: ARM rates in DFW running only 30-40 bps below 30yr fixed — a narrow spread. When the
  gap is only 0.25-0.375%, the case for an ARM weakens versus wider-spread periods.
- National MBA data (Jan 2026): ARM applications up 113.1% YoY, now ~7.7% of all applications.
- Today's ARMs are indexed to SOFR; lenders typically add a 2.50-3.00 point margin.
- Redfin analysis (Mar 2026): typical buyer saves ~$150/month with an ARM vs 30yr fixed nationally —
  useful national baseline, must be reframed with DFW-specific numbers, not repeated as-is.
