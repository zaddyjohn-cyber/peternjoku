## ⚠️ DATA FLAG — verify before publishing
Initial assumption was FICO 10T is already usable for FHA loans in 2026. Two independent searches
confirm this is WRONG: **FICO Score 10T becomes available for FHA-approved lenders to use starting
January 1, 2027** (HUD/FHFA announcement). In 2026, the Enterprises (Fannie/Freddie) are in a
limited rollout of VantageScore 4.0 only; FICO 10T deliveries to the GSEs also aren't broadly live
yet. The existing site post `vantagescore-4-mortgage-credit-score-texas-2026.html` correctly covers
VantageScore 4.0 — this new post must NOT claim FICO 10T is usable today. Frame it as: "starts
January 1, 2027 — here's what Texas FHA buyers should know now, in 2026, to get ahead of it."

## Verified facts
- HUD announced adoption of FICO 10T and VantageScore 4.0 for FHA loans; called it "an important
  milestone" in credit-score modernization (April 2026 FHFA/HUD announcement).
- FICO 10T uses trended credit data (24 months of balance/payment history, not just a snapshot) plus
  rental payment data where reported.
- Effective date for FHA-approved lender use: January 1, 2027.
- Until then, FHA lenders continue using Classic FICO from all three bureaus.
- FHA's existing baseline credit guideline unchanged: 580 mid-score for 3.5% down; 500-579 requires
  10% down.
