## Verified facts to build on

**TDI release, 2026-09-29** (fetched directly from tdi.texas.gov):
- TDI is **clarifying its rule** that "insurance companies can't decline or not renew a home insurance
  policy based on the age of a house or its roof."
- Further language: "companies can't deny or not renew a policy simply because the house or any of these
  materials are older."
- Carriers **may still** base decisions on the home's physical **condition**, including its components.
- Insurance Commissioner **Amanda Crawford**: "Texans deserve fair access to home insurance, no matter
  the age of their home."
- **No effective date, docket number or comment period is stated in the release.** The linked rule file
  is named `211006proposal.pdf`. Treat as a clarification published that date; tell readers to confirm
  current status with TDI or their carrier.

Related, already on the site: TDI rate filings averaging **−4.3%** (per
`texas-homeowners-insurance-rates-dropping-escrow-2026.html`, 2026-10-08).

Rate benchmark: PMMS **7.32%** 30-yr, week of 2026-10-22.
