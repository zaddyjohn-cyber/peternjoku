## ⚠️ DATA FLAG — resolved 2026-10-08

**SKILL.md Tier 10 item 5 said the GNND search has an "only Mon–Wed exclusive window." That is
wrong and was not written into the post.** Two WebSearch passes found no support for any
Monday–Wednesday window for GNND. Every current source describes a **7-day exclusive listing
period** for GNND properties on HUDHomeStore. (A police-officer affiliate page says 5 days and one
older source describes a separate 7-day lottery period preceding the exclusive listing period —
the rules have changed over time, so the post tells readers to confirm the live deadline on the
listing itself rather than planning around a fixed window.)

The "Mon–Wed" idea appears to be a conflation with the **owner-occupant exclusivity windows on
ordinary HUD REO listings**, which is a different program. SKILL.md corrected in this run.

Confirmed and used in the post:
- 50% discount off **list price**, delivered as a **silent second mortgage** — no payments, no
  interest, forgiven over the 36-month occupancy period
- **36 consecutive months** owner occupancy required
- Bid must be submitted by a **HUD-registered broker**, electronically, at the **full list price**
- Ties broken by **random lottery**, selected within ~2 business days of the listing closing
- **Earnest money due within 2 business days** of winning, by cashier's check, certified check or
  money order
- Eligible: pre-K–12 teachers, firefighters, EMTs, law enforcement officers — full-time, with the
  required geographic/jurisdictional tie
- Properties are **HUD REO homes in designated revitalization areas only**
- FHA financing permitted; down payment computed on the discounted (50%) amount

Nurse clarification retained from SKILL.md: **"Nurse Next Door" is a separate, non-HUD commercial
program** and is not GNND. Nurses are not a GNND-eligible category.
