## Key Data Points (from search results)
- Owelty lien allows buyout refi up to 95% LTV (vs 80% cap on cash-out refi)
- Texas Art. XVI Sec. 50(a)(3) governs homestead owelty liens
- Divorce decree alone does NOT remove spouse from mortgage
- Alimony/child support income: counts if 3+ years remaining, documented in decree
- Pre-approval before decree finalization is best practice
