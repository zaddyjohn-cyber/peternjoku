## Key Data Points
- Earnest money: 1-3% of purchase price (DFW: 1-2% typical)
- DFW median ~$380K → typical earnest money $3,800–$7,600
- Delivery deadline: 3 business days after effective date (to title company)
- Option fee: $100-500 standard; $750-1,000+ competitive DFW submarkets (Frisco, McKinney)
- Option fee delivery: 3 days after effective date (to SELLER directly, not title company — common confusion)
- Option period: 5-10 calendar days (7 most common)
- Termination deadline: 5:00 PM local time, last day of option period
