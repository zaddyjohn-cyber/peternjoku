## Primary source read in full (Federal Register 2026-13834, Docket CFPB-2026-0018)
- Published 2026-07-09; comments due 30 days after publication (Aug 10, 2026). 22 numbered questions.
- Scope: TRID, TILA right of rescission, reverse mortgage disclosures. ATR/QM is in EO Sec. 2 but NOT in this RFI.
- EO 14393 signed 2026-03-13 (91 FR 13203, Mar 18, 2026). Sec 2(i) tailor ATR/QM for smaller banks incl. a possible broader portfolio-QM safe harbor; 2(ii) replace TRID timing with a materiality-based standard; 2(vii) exempt rate-and-term (incl. cash-out) refinances from rescission.
- Signed by Russell Vought, Acting Director.
