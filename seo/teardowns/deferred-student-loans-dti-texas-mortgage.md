## Facts cross-checked (use these)
- Deferred/forbearance student loans ARE counted in DTI even at $0 reported payment — they are
  never simply ignored.
- FHA: if credit report shows $0 payment, lender must use 0.5% of outstanding balance as the
  monthly qualifying payment.
- Conventional (Fannie Mae): if $0 payment, use 1% of outstanding balance (or the fully amortizing
  payment on the credit report if lower and documented).
- VA: VA guidance generally allows using 5% of the balance divided by 12 IF verified deferment
  extends 12+ months past closing, otherwise similar 1%-style estimate applies — lenders vary,
  frame as "ask your loan officer to verify your specific case," don't overclaim a single VA %.
- USDA: similar to conventional — 0.5% of balance is commonly used when actual payment is $0,
  though USDA underwriting can also accept the documented IBR/IDR payment reported on the credit
  report if verified.
- IDR/IBR enrollment lowering the *reported* payment (even $0 or low) can still trigger the %-of-
  balance calculation above — the fix is documentation, not enrollment alone.
