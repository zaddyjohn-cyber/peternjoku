## Verified Data
- Conventional: 45% max (50% with compensating factors)
- FHA: 43–50% standard (up to 57% per some lenders with AUS approval)
- VA: 41% benchmark (residual income is the real qualifier)
- USDA: 41% (29% front-end preferred)
- Texas income to spend <30% on housing: $93,982/yr (Reventure App)
- DFW median SFH price: ~$410K
- Texas property taxes: 1.5–2.2% effective rate by county
