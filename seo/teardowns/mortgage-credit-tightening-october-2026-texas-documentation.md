## GAP SHEET — our post must cover

1. The number: MBA **Mortgage Credit Availability Index = 107.1 in September 2026**, down 0.2%
   from August's 107.3 (MBA release dated 2026-10-08). Higher index = looser credit; the index
   is benchmarked to 100 = March 2012.
2. The split: **Conventional MCAI −0.4%**, **Government MCAI unchanged (0.0%)**, **Jumbo down a
   second consecutive month**. Non-agency/non-QM growth is partially offsetting jumbo.
3. Joel Kan (MBA VP & deputy chief economist) attribution: lenders tightened **documentation
   requirements on conventional loans** and **reduced cash-out refinance and investor-purchase
   offerings**.
4. COMPARISON TABLE: what tightened vs what didn't — Conventional / FHA-VA-USDA (Government) /
   Jumbo / Non-QM, across direction, what changed, who it hits, what to do.
5. Borrower document checklist, concrete: 2 months full bank statements (all pages), 30-day
   paystubs, 2 yrs W-2 or 1099 + 2 yrs returns if self-employed, written explanation for any
   large deposit, gift letter + donor's statement, reserve documentation, lease + rent roll for
   investment property, LOE for recent credit events, and the **pre-closing credit re-pull**.
6. The Texas-specific consequence: tightening on cash-out hits Texas hardest because
   **50(a)(6)** already caps at 80% CLTV and requires a 12-day cooling-off notice — fewer
   lender programs on top of that is a real constraint. Point readers to the 50(a)(6) post.
7. Why **FHA is the stable door right now** (Government MCAI flat) — and the 580/3.5% route.
8. Payment math at 7.40% to show why reserves matter now.
9. Named composite scenario — Mesquite buyer whose conventional file got a second document
   request, moved to FHA, closed.
10. 4 FAQs with NMLS + phone.
