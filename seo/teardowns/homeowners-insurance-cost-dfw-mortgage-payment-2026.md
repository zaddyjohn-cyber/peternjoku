# Teardown: "DFW homeowners insurance mortgage payment 2026" / "how insurance affects DTI Texas"

## Verified facts (WebSearch, 2026-09-19 — Dallas Fed research (dallasfed.org/research/swe/2026),
Richey Insurance stats, Latent Insurance DFW page, SellingNTX/Compass realtor page, TAP Insurance)
- Statewide TX average premium: <$2,000 (2020) -> >$3,500 (2026), a 79% rise in six years
- 2022->2023: ~21% increase; 2024: additional ~19% increase; 2026: another 18-28% increase on top of
  those (compounding, not a one-time jump)
- Dallas-specific 2026 premiums: roughly $5,400-$6,300/year
- Fort Worth-specific 2026 average: ~$5,458/year
- DFW metroplex absorbed $1B+ in hail-related insured losses in the 2024 season alone — cited as the
  primary driver of repricing
- Wind/hail deductible in most TX policies is a % of dwelling value, not a flat dollar amount —
  example: $350,000 insured home at 2% deductible = $7,000 out of pocket before coverage kicks in
- Front-end DTI (housing-cost ratio) includes taxes, insurance, PMI/MIP, HOA — not just P&I; most
  lenders target 28% front-end, hard cap near 43% total DTI in Texas
- Rising premiums are pushing some homeowners to drop or reduce coverage — a red flag for lenders,
  since most require insurance to stay escrowed and current
