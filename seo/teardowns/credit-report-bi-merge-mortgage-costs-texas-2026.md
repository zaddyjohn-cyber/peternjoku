## Data Points
- Credit report cost: ~$50 (2022) → ~$540 (2026) — CHLA estimate
- FHFA directed Fannie/Freddie to approve ALL lenders for VantageScore 4.0 (Sept 2026)
- Currently: tri-merge (Equifax + Experian + TransUnion), middle score used
- Bi-merge: two bureaus, lower of two used for qualifying
- Pulte quote: "Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion have been overcharging Americans for too long"
- Pulte called bureaus "cartel-like"
- FICO 10T: approved for FHA starting Jan 1, 2027 (announced, not yet live)
- VantageScore 4.0: already in use by Fannie/Freddie for all lenders
- Single-bureau: still under study — no implementation date yet
