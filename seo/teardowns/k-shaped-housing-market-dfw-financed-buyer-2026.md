## Strongest competitor structure (better.com + rocketmortgage, combined)

- Fully underwritten pre-approval vs pre-qualification
- Flexible closing date / seller rent-back ("45 or 60 days may beat a cash 21-day close")
- Escalation clause with a cap
- Appraisal gap coverage
- Larger earnest money (5%+)
- Limit contingencies to deal-breakers
- Lender "cash-backed"/upfront-commitment programs
- Approx 900–1,400 words, no tables, no FAQ schema on most, no local numbers

## Competitor weaknesses to exploit

1. **Zero Texas contract mechanics.** None mentions the Texas Third Party Financing Addendum
   (TPFA), the option fee / termination option, or that Texas earnest money and option fee are
   two separate checks. This is the single biggest gap — the whole "limit your contingencies"
   advice is wrong-shaped in Texas because the TPFA and the option period work differently.
2. **No October 2026 data.** All pages are evergreen. None cites 7.40% PMMS, the −16% YoY
   purchase-application drop, or the K-shaped split.
3. **No DFW numbers** — no median price, no county tax rate, no payment math.
4. **No named LO scenario.**
5. **Reckless advice risk:** several suggest "waive contingencies." Our post should explicitly
   warn against waiving the TPFA in Texas and explain what the buyer actually loses.
6. **No comparison table.** Not one competitor page has a table.

## GAP SHEET — our post must cover

1. What "K-shaped housing market" means (CNN, Oct 15 2026) — high-income/cash buyers growing
   share while rate-dependent buyers exit.
2. The Oct 2026 numbers: PMMS 30-yr **7.40%** week of Oct 15 (unchanged WoW, highest since Nov
   2023; 15-yr 6.71%; year-ago 6.27–6.30%). MBA: purchase applications **−16% YoY**; MBA contract
   rate 7.50% (week ended Oct 9) — highest since Nov 2023; ARM share 10.5% of applications.

   financed offers in 2025 — frame as "this is the gap you are closing."
