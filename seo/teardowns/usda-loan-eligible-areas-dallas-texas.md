## ⚠️ DATA FLAG — RESOLVED 2026-09-03
Competitors were all wrong for DFW. Verified direct from eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov
(Kaufman, Rockwall, Ellis counties → "Dallas, TX HUD Metro FMR Area", guidelines in effect
as of 09-03-2026):

| | 1–4 person | 5–8 person |
|---|---|---|
| **USDA Guaranteed 502 — DFW metro** | **$139,300** | **$183,900** |
| USDA Direct 502 — DFW metro | $96,900 | $127,950 |

- greysq / coast2coast / griffinfunding cite $119,850 / $158,250 = the *national floor*, stale.
- neighborsbank / dsld / usdaloans.com cite $122,800 / $162,100 = the *current* national floor.
- Our SKILL.md previously said $112,450 / $148,450 = two years stale. **Now fixed.**
- DFW sits ABOVE the floor because the Dallas MSA median income is high. Every USDA-eligible
  county in the DFW MSA shares $139,300 / $183,900. Outlying non-MSA counties (Navarro,
  Henderson) use the lower floor — check individually.
- Our post should state the $139,300 / $183,900 figure with the USDA tool as the cited source,
  and explicitly say lender sites quoting $119,850 are quoting the wrong (national) number.
