## Search-aggregated consensus on the exceptions (cross-checked across usdaloans.com, neighborsbank.com, societymortgage.com, missourimortgagesource.com, freeandclear.com — freeandclear/usdaloans.com timed out on direct fetch but corroborated via search snippet)
- USDA is primary-residence-only; cannot be used for vacation home, second home, or investment/rental property.
- Exception path: buyer may already own a home AND still qualify for a new USDA loan if:
  1. Relocating for work/employment beyond a reasonable commuting distance from the current home
  2. Current home is no longer structurally adequate and cannot be made adequate cost-effectively (manufactured home not on permanent foundation, overcrowding ~1.5+ people/room, no accommodation for a disabled household member)
  3. Separating households (divorce, or a non-occupying co-borrower wanting their own dwelling)
  4. Building/buying a new residence with a documented plan to sell the current one
- Cannot have two active USDA-guaranteed/direct loans at once; the currently-owned home cannot itself be financed by a Section 502/504 USDA loan or active USDA grant.
- Lender requires documentation: job offer/relocation letter, divorce decree, inspection/appraisal showing inadequacy, listing agreement for the home being sold.
- Both mortgage payments (existing + new) must qualify under DTI if the old home is being kept, not sold.
