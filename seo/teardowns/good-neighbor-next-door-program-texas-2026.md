## Verified Data
- 50% off HUD-listed REO homes
- Eligible: K-12 teachers, law enforcement, firefighters, EMTs only
- Nurses: NOT eligible (Nurse Next Door is a different program)
- Requirements: work in area, FTHB (no home in 12 months), live there 36 months
- Silent second = 50% of purchase price, forgiven at 36 months
- FHA-eligible: 3.5% of discounted price
- Texas availability: yes, via hudhomestore.com (filter by state = TX)
- Weekly listing rotation: Mon–Wed each week
