## Strongest competitor: mortgagelaw.com memo (2020)
Six requirements: (1) 1 year since the prior home equity loan closed, (2) can pay off the home equity loan + a purchase-money first, (3) no cash back (actual costs/reserves may be rolled in), (4) CLTV at or below 80%, (5) notice no more than 3 business days after application and at least 12 days before closing, (6) 50(f-1) affidavit at closing.
Regulator notice: converting gives up home equity protections (lender may foreclose without a court order; loan becomes recourse).
