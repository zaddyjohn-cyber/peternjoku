## Confirmed Facts
- Policy: FHFA directive to Fannie/Freddie servicers
- Effective: Oct 1, 2026 (servicers could implement as early as April 27, 2026)
- Old rule: Must evaluate borrower for home retention (loan mod, forbearance, repayment plan) BEFORE short sale
- New rule: Servicers may offer short sale for ANY eligible hardship, no home retention eval first
- Applies to: Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac conventional loans ONLY (not FHA, VA, USDA)
- Short sale waiting period: 4 years before next conventional; 3 years FHA; 2 years VA
- Texas deficiency judgment: lender CAN pursue deficiency unless waived in agreement
- DFW short sale timeline: 60-120 days approval typically
- 7%+ rates = more homeowners unable to sell (underwater at 2021/2022 values in some cases)
