## Verified Facts
- November 2025: Texas voters approved Prop 13 raising school homestead exemption $100K → $140K
- Effective: 2026 tax year (applies to taxes paid/escrowed in 2026)
- Additional over-65/disabled exemption: $60K additional school exemption (total $200K)
- School district effective tax rate statewide: ~1.05% (varies by ISD)

## Savings Math ($140K exemption at common DFW tax rates)
- Dallas County (~2.0%): $140K × 2.0% = $2,800/yr savings = $233/mo escrow reduction
- Collin County (~1.5%): $140K × 1.5% = $2,100/yr = $175/mo
- Tarrant County (~2.2%): $140K × 2.2% = $3,080/yr = $257/mo
- Rockwall County (~1.8%): $140K × 1.8% = $2,520/yr = $210/mo
- Kaufman County (~1.9%): $140K × 1.9% = $2,660/yr = $222/mo

Note: above are total rate savings (school + city/county components all use the exemption). School-only portion at ~1.05% = $140K × 1.05% = $1,470/yr, but most county rates apply the exemption to all taxing entities.
