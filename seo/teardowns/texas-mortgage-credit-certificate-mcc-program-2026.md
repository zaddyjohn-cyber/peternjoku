## ⚠️ DATA FLAG
Competitor shows Texas MCC rate at 30% with purchase limit $381,308. But:
- TDHCA official doc says "20% of mortgage interest paid"
- Another source says "15%"
- The MCC rate varies by bond issue and TDHCA program — TDHCA sets the rate per issuance, not a fixed state-wide number
- $381,308 purchase price cap seems low for DFW (median $440K+); TDHCA may have DFW-specific targeted areas with higher limits
- RESOLUTION: State the credit rate as "15-20% of annual mortgage interest paid, capped at $2,000/year" and note the exact rate depends on which TDHCA bond issuance you're enrolled in. Do NOT state 30% — competitor may be citing an older program or a different HFA.
- The income limit of $137,800 may be for a specific county size; verify against TDHCA county-specific limits for DFW.
