## Key Verified Facts

- Circular: VA Circular 26-24-14 (issued June 11, 2024, effective Aug 10, 2024)
- Changed 1: Still in effect as of 2026 as "temporary local variance until rescinded"
- What it allows: Veterans may pay "reasonable and customary" buyer-broker charges on VA-guaranteed purchases
- Cannot be financed: Must pay cash at closing (does not add to loan balance)
- Must appear: Section H, "Other" on Closing Disclosure
- Liquid assets: Charge must be factored into liquid assets analysis
- TX typical buyer-broker fee: 2.5–3.0% of purchase price (pre-Aug 2024: seller often paid)
- On $350K home: 3% = $10,500 — significant cash requirement
- DTI impact: Does NOT affect monthly DTI (it's a closing cost, not ongoing payment)
- VA loan still: zero down, no PMI, all other VA benefits unchanged
