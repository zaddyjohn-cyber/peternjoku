## Verified facts to build on

**The 2026 waiver** (WBK + tenaco, both fetched):
- Title: **"Waiver to Individual Water Well Distance Requirements,"** against Single Family Housing
  Policy Handbook **4000.1**. No Mortgagee Letter number and no separate waiver number is published.
- **Effective immediately.** Reported 2026-07-27 (tenaco) / posted 2026-07-30 (WBK).
- **Existing construction only** — does NOT extend to minimum property standards for new construction.
- Two conditions still apply: (i) the property meets the **local jurisdiction's** distance requirements,
  and (ii) the mortgagee **documents acceptable water testing results**.
- "Local jurisdiction" = the local health authority, environmental agency, or governing municipality that
  **inspected, permitted, or grandfathered** the private water system *in its current configuration*.

**Pre-waiver HUD baseline distances** (HUD HOC Reference Guide):
- Well to property line: **10 ft**
- Well to septic tank: **50 ft**
- Well to septic drain field: **100 ft**, reducible to **75 ft** where local authorities allow
- "Either the health authority having jurisdiction or HUD may increase these distances."
- HUD also allows shorter local distances if the lender puts evidence of local compliance in the case binder.

**Water testing:** a state-certified lab using EPA-approved methods; at minimum coliform, nitrate,
nitrite and lead (vendor source — attribute, do not state as the universal panel). A HUD-hosted guide
says test per current local and state private-well regulations including all microbiological and
chemical parameters. A failing result must be corrected (disinfection, treatment system) and re-tested
before closing.

**Texas specifics:** a Texas real-estate source says Texas requires the septic system to sit 50–100 ft
from a private well depending on soil conditions and the permitted system type, and that FHA and VA
lenders can require a licensed septic inspection before clear-to-close. **Attribute as reported** — not
confirmed against TCEQ.
