# Teardown: "VA IRRRL funding fee increase 2026" / "VA refinance fee bill"

## Verified facts (WebSearch, 2026-09-19, two independent passes — Stars and Stripes 2026-06-15,
Army/Air Force Times 2026-09-15, National Mortgage News, mortgage-maestro.com)
- Bill: H.R. 9237, "Take Care of America's Veterans Act" (incorporates the Sharri Briley and Eric
  Edmundson Veterans Benefits Expansion Act, H.R. 6047)
- IRRRL funding fee: 0.50% -> 1.42% for most non-exempt veterans (nearly triples)
- VA loan assumption fee: 0.50% -> 1.00% (doubles)
- Exempt: veterans/surviving spouses with compensable service-connected disability rating, certain
  Purple Heart recipients, qualifying pre-discharge disability ratings — stays exempt under the bill
- Example cited: $325,000 loan, current fee $1,625 (0.5%) vs proposed $4,615 (1.42%)
- Status as of 2026-09-15: pulled from House floor consideration twice, motion to recommit failed
  210-211 on July 16, no confirmed vote date
- Purpose: CBO projects ~$4B in net direct spending reduction through 2036, funds expanded benefits
  for severely injured veterans needing in-home care
- NOT yet law — must frame as proposed/pending, not enacted
