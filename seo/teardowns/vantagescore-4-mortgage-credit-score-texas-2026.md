# Teardown: "VantageScore 4.0 mortgage credit score" / "new credit score mortgage 2026"

## Verified facts (WebSearch, 2026-09-19, CNBC 2026-09-15 + 2026-05-05, VantageScore press releases,
ABA Banking Journal 2026-04, FHFA official release)
- April 22, 2026: FHFA announced VantageScore 4.0 approved for loans sold to Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac
- Limited rollout through summer 2026 (approved lenders only, prior written approval required)
- **Sept 9, 2026**: expanded to ALL approved lenders, no prior written approval needed — this is the
  fresh, dated hook
- Uses ~400% more data than legacy FICO scoring; considers alternative data (rent payments)
- Scores ~33 million more U.S. adults than FICO Classic/FICO 10T; ~4.9 million of those are
  estimated mortgage-eligible
- As of Aug 31, 2026: VantageScore 4.0 was the sole score used for >9% of all Fannie/Freddie-
  securitized mortgages since May 1, 2026
- Adopted by FHA, VA, Federal Home Loan Banks, Rocket Mortgage among others
- Lenders now CHOOSE between VantageScore 4.0 and legacy FICO — not a forced replacement (as of this
  writing); borrowers may be scored differently by different lenders
