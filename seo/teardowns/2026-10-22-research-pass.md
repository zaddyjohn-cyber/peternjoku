scenario, no table of old vs. new.

sanitary, structurally sound; roof, HVAC, water, foundation); 4) Texas option period — inspection still matters;
5) radon in North Texas — low-priority but still buyer's choice; 6) sheds/detached garages common on Forney/Terrell
acreage; 7) S. 4218 VA Appraisal Modernization Act is a bill, not law; 8) composite Garland scenario; 9) 4 FAQs.
