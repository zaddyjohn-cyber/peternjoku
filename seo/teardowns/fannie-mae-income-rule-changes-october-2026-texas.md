## Primary source read in full
Fannie Mae SEL-2026-09, dated Oct 7, 2026 (3 pages):
- Income continuance: income with a defined expiration date must continue at least 3 years from the **application date instead of the note date**. Effective immediately. Impacted topics include B3-3.4-02 (alimony/child support), Social Security, VA benefits, trust, notes receivable, royalty, public assistance, mortgage differential, RSU income.
- Employment offers/contracts: acceptance may be shown by signed agreement, written confirmation (e.g. email) or other verifiable means.
- Verbal VOE: specific methods removed; timing unchanged.
- AOLs: removed the "commonly accepted by private investors" requirement and the Texas 50(a)(6) and power-of-attorney exclusions.
- Tenant ROFR arising from statute/ordinance does not make a loan ineligible.
- K-1 <25% ownership: verbal VOE required for applications dated on/after Dec 2, 2026; not required when the K-1 shows only rental income.
