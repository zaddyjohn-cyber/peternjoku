## Verified Data
- CD required 3 business days before closing (TRID rule, effective Oct 3, 2015)
- 5 pages: Page 1 (loan terms + projected payments + cash to close), Page 2 (closing cost details), Page 3 (cash to close calculation), Page 4 (loan disclosures), Page 5 (loan calculations + contact info)
- Texas title policy: typically 0.5–0.7% of purchase price
- Escrow pre-fund: 2–3 months of taxes + insurance
