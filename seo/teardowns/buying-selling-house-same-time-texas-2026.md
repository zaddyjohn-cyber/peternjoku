## Data points to use
- Home sale contingency weakens offers in competitive/fast-moving markets; DFW move-up price points
  average under 40 days on market.
- Bridge loan: needs 10-15% equity in current home; lender must qualify borrower to carry both
  payments; origination 1.5-3% + 8-10% annual interest while carrying two mortgages.
- Typical timeline: 45-75 days from listing current home to closing both.
- HELOC as an alternative to a bridge loan for the down payment on the new home.
