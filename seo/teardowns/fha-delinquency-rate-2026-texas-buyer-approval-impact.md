## GAP SHEET — our post must cover

1. The numbers, from MBA's National Delinquency Survey, **Q2 2026** (released 2026-08-13):
   - FHA total delinquency **11.79%**, down **9 bps** from Q1 (Q1 was 11.88%), but **+122 bps**
     year over year.
   - VA **4.89%**, down 10 bps quarter over quarter, **+57 bps** YoY.
   - Conventional **2.72%**, up 12 bps quarter over quarter.
   - FHA **serious** delinquency (90+ days or in foreclosure) up **more than 225 bps** YoY,
     versus roughly 6 bps for conventional and 31 bps for VA.
2. COMPARISON TABLE: FHA vs VA vs conventional — total delinquency rate, QoQ change, YoY change,
   serious-delinquency YoY change, and what it means for a borrower in that program.
3. **Why FHA runs higher** — honest explanation, not a scare: FHA is designed for 3.5% down and
   580+ FICO, so by construction its borrowers carry thinner equity and thinner reserves. A
   higher delinquency rate is the expected cost of an access program, not evidence the program
   is failing.
4. **Does this change my approval?** The key pairing: MBA's **Government MCAI was unchanged in
   September 2026** while the Conventional MCAI fell 0.4% — FHA/VA/USDA credit availability did
   NOT tighten. Lender **overlays** are where a borrower may feel it (score, DTI, reserves,
   recent lates, collections), and overlays vary by lender — which is the practical reason to
   work a file with someone who knows which investors are strict.
5. **The MMIF counterweight:** record **$189B**, **11.45%** capital ratio, 5x+ the 2% statutory
   minimum; stress-tested to **4.42%** in a Great-Recession-scale downturn — still above the
   minimum. FHA is not going away and is not being pulled back.
6. **Texas-specific:** Texas was among the states with the largest quarterly delinquency
   increases in Q3 2025. ICE reported Texas and Florida together account for **39%** of
   underwater homes nationwide — driven by 2022–2024 minimal-down purchases in fast-appreciating
   metros meeting flat prices. Tie to DFW: prices roughly flat to slightly down YoY in 2026,
   so a 3.5%-down 2026 purchase has very little equity cushion for 2–3 years.
7. **How not to become the statistic** — the practical core, with math at 7.40%:
   - Reserves: 3–6 months PITI even when FHA doesn't require it on a 1-unit.
   - Escrow shock: Texas taxes + insurance are the usual cause. Show the Dallas County 2.0%
     example on a $300K home ($500/mo tax escrow) and why a first-year escrow estimate on a
     brand-new build or a seller's homestead-exempt tax bill under-collects.
   - DTI buffer: qualify under the max, not at it.
   - Don't take the car loan (link the car-loan post).
   - Know loss mitigation BEFORE you need it — FHA partial claim / loss mit (link).
8. Named composite scenario — Mesquite FHA buyer whose escrow jumped at the first re-analysis,
   and what the reserve cushion did.

## ⚠️ DATA FLAGS

- **FHA 11.79% / VA 4.89% / conventional 2.72% / Q1 11.88%: verified**, MBA National Delinquency
  Survey Q2 2026 release dated 2026-08-13 (primary source) plus HousingWire and NMP coverage.
  Safe to publish. These are **Q2 2026** — Q3 2026 is not out yet (normally November), so the
  post must label the quarter and note Q3 is pending.
- Serious-delinquency YoY: sources give "more than 225 bps" and "227 bps." Use **"more than 225
  basis points"** and attribute to MBA. Do not pick 227 as if precise.
- MMIF $189B / 11.45% / 4.42% stress result: from Inman (Jan 2026) reporting on the FHA annual
  report to Congress. Secondary source — attribute to FHA's annual report via Inman, and note
  the next annual report is due around November 2026.
- ICE "Texas and Florida = 39% of underwater homes": secondary (ICE Mortgage Monitor via
  HousingWire). Attribute to ICE. Do not extrapolate a DFW-specific underwater share — no such
  figure was found.
- The Houston figure (1,583 of 50,627 FHA loans 90+ days past due as of Aug 2026) covers **one
  origination cohort**, not all Houston FHA loans. **Do not publish it** — too easy to misread
  as a metro delinquency rate, and Houston is outside Peter's market anyway.
- **Do NOT use** americandefault.org's "FHA is 6.3x conventional" or "foreclosure filings up
  15%" — aggregator site, and 11.79 / 2.72 = 4.3x, not 6.3x, so the claim does not even match
  the MBA data. Our post computes the ratio from the MBA figures instead.
- Reject the "coming wave" / "gathering storm" framing. The brand voice is calm and practical.
