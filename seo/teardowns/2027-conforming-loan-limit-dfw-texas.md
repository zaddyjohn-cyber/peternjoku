## Verified facts (WebSearch 2026-09-29)
- 2026 baseline one-unit conforming limit: $832,750, announced by FHFA Nov 25, 2025.
- 2026 high-cost ceiling: $1,249,125 (confirmed in VA loan-limit coverage).
- Early lender-set 2027 limits: $845,000 (multiple lenders); one source cites $847,440.
- Calculated Risk: based on year-over-year house-price change through May, the 2027 CLL would be
  close to $850,000.
- Official FHFA 2027 announcement expected late November 2026. NOT yet announced.
- FHA Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington MSA 2026 limit: $563,500, one figure for the whole MSA.
