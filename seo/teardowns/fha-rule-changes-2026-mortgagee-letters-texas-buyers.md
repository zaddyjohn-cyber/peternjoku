## GAP SHEET — what our post must cover

Must match competitors (the factual spine):
1. **EO 14393, "Promoting Access to Mortgage Credit," signed 2026-03-13** — the policy driver
2. **2026-06-23: HUD announced 14 policy changes** to the FHA Single Family program in a batch of
   Mortgagee Letters, effective immediately except where noted
3. **ML 2026-06 — Limited 203(k) draws:** max **four** draws per contractor (initial at closing, up
   to two intermediate, final), raised from two; up to **two disbursements per draw**, still counting
   as one draw; work needing more than four draws per contractor is a "major" repair and ineligible.
   **Standard 203(k)** clarified: multiple disbursements allowed per draw, max **five** draws.
   HUD's stated reason: the 2024 Limited 203(k) ceiling increase **$35,000 → $75,000** (with a longer
   rehab period) was not matched by a draw-structure change, putting the MMI Fund at risk.
4. **ML 2026-07** — rescinds the Important Notice to Homebuyers requirement (**Form HUD-92900-B**)
5. **ML 2026-08 — loss mitigation:** became **mandatory for servicers 2026-09-21**; changed trial
   payment plan rules and limits pre-foreclosure re-review requests to cases where a change in the
   borrower's circumstances affects eligibility
6. **ML 2026-09** — "Eliminating Unnecessary Requirements for FHA Mortgagee Approval and Quality
   Control": removes the staff-access-to-guidance training requirement; creates a **permanent
   exception** to the Early Payment Default QC sample requirement for certain mortgages in
   Presidentially-Declared Major Disaster Areas (no per-disaster waiver needed)
7. **ML 2026-10** — updates QC requirements for appraisal field reviews
8. **ML 2026-02 (2026-01-22)** — formalizes requirements for sale of a beneficial interest in a
   group of mortgages and Declaration of Trust submission
9. All updates to be folded into a forthcoming **Handbook 4000.1** revision

Gaps to fill (our advantage):
10. **Triage table: "Will you notice this?"** — each ML × who it affects (buyer / servicer /
    lender back office) × what the borrower actually experiences. This is the centerpiece.
11. **203(k) draw math worked for a DFW renovation** — $75K Limited 203(k) across four draws vs the
    old two, on a Garland or Mesquite fixer-upper, with the 2026 FHA limit of $563,500 as the
    purchase ceiling
12. **What ML 2026-08 means for a Texas homeowner already behind** — and the practical consequence
    of the re-review limitation (get the hardship documented the first time)
13. **What the HUD-92900-B rescission changes at the closing table** (one fewer disclosure; the
    substantive protections live in the CD and the appraisal, which are unchanged)
14. **What did NOT change** — FHA minimum 580 FICO / 3.5% down, the $563,500 DFW FHA limit, MIP
    structure, DPA stacking. Readers will assume "14 changes" means their loan terms moved. They
    didn't. Saying so plainly is high-value.
15. **Named composite scenario** — Mesquite 203(k) buyer using the new four-draw structure
16. 4 FAQs with NMLS + phone

## ⚠️ DATA FLAGS
- **FHA limit stays $563,500** for the whole Dallas–Fort Worth–Arlington MSA (CLAUDE.md confirmed).
  Nothing in the 2026 ML batch changes loan limits. Do not imply otherwise.
- ML 2026-07's operative text was not retrieved directly — only its title/summary via secondary
  sources. Describe it at the level the sources support (rescinds the HUD-92900-B notice
  requirement) and do not invent detail about replacement disclosures.
- ML 2026-10's details were not retrieved. Mention it by title and audience (lender QC) only.
- The **Partial Claim Reinstatement Advance Payment (RAP)** published 2026-07-20 is a **DRAFT**
  mortgagee letter, not final. Either omit it or label it clearly as proposed. Do **not** confuse it
  with the student-loan Repayment Assistance Plan covered in
  blog/student-loan-rap-plan-mortgage-dti-texas-2026.html — unrelated program, same acronym.
- "14 policy changes" comes from the Consumer Finance Monitor summary of HUD's 2026-06-23
  announcement; our post names only the letters that were actually verified. Do not enumerate 14
  items when 6 are confirmed.
- ML numbering/titles vary slightly between secondary sources. Use HUD's own index wording where
  quoted and attribute analysis to the law-firm summaries rather than asserting it as HUD's text.
