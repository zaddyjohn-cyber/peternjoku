## Verified facts (2026)
- General homestead exemption for school district taxes: **$140,000** off taxable value (all homeowners) — this is the 2026 figure, not the older $40,000 figure.
- Over-65 or disabled: additional $60,000 school district exemption (combined $200,000), plus a tax ceiling frozen at the year they turned 65/became disabled.
- Counties/cities may offer up to an additional 20% optional exemption.
- Filing deadline: **April 30** of the tax year (rolls to next business day if it falls on a weekend).
- New homebuyers: since 2022, you can claim the exemption prorated from your closing date for the rest of the purchase year, as long as the previous owner didn't already have homestead exemption on the property that year — file immediately after closing, no need to wait for the next April 30 cycle.
- Late filing allowed up to **2 years** after the delinquency date, with a refund of overpaid taxes.
