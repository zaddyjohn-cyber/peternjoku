## Data points to use (verified via WebSearch, cite loosely — no single authoritative source page,
these are consistent across LRG/Redfin/calclogix/Kinne):
- MUD tax range: $200-$400+/mo added to payment
- Rate example: $450K home, $0.25/$100 = ~$94/mo vs $0.85/$100 = ~$319/mo (a $225/mo swing)
- Buying power reduction: $30,000-$70,000 less home a buyer qualifies for
- MUD bonds typically retire over 20-30 years, then the tax often steps down or disappears
- Flows through escrow like property tax; factors into DTI at qualification
